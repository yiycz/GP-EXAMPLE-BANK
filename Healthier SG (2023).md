## Healthier SG (2023)

The government introduced Healthier SG to encourage residents aged 40 and above to enrol with family doctors for personalised health plans, subsidised screenings and preventive care, reducing the burden of chronic diseases associated with ageing.

**Use:** The initiative has shown promising results, with 1.5 million residents enrolled by August 2026 and chronic disease screening participation rising from 62.6% in 2023 to 71.5% in 2025, although its long-term effectiveness in reducing healthcare costs remains to be established.
