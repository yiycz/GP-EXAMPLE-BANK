## SG Child Support Package

The government announced a new package offering up to S$62,000 in support per Singaporean child from birth to age 17, replacing the Baby Bonus and Large Families schemes, with most payouts beginning in April 2027.

The package could alleviate the financial burden of raising children, but its effectiveness remains unproven, especially since decisions about parenthood are also influenced by personal aspirations, career priorities and social expectations.
