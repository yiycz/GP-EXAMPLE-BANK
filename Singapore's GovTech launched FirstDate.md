## Singapore's GovTech launched FirstDate

Singapore's GovTech launched FirstDate, a government-backed dating platform for unmarried public officers aged 21–35, using Singpass verification and the Gale–Shapley matching algorithm to recommend compatible partners and encourage meaningful romantic relationships. 

Effectiveness: The initiative could facilitate marriage by reducing barriers to finding compatible partners, but its effectiveness remains unproven as it is still in its pilot stage and does not address structural obstacles to marriage and parenthood, such as high living costs and demanding work schedules.
