## The art of Charile Chan Hock Chye

The art of Charile Chan Hock Chye provides an alternative narrative of Singapore's background as well as a satirical version of Singapore's political history. 

It is graphic novel btw. 

This novel initially received funding from NAC, which later rescinded as it risks undermining government's legitimacy. 

**Use:** Government may favor pro-government arts.
