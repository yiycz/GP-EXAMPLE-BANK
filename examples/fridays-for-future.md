## Fridays for Future

Greta Thunberg's lone 2018 school strike grew, through social media, into Fridays for Future. By 2019 it drew millions of young people to climate strikes in over 100 countries.

**Use:** This supports the argument that social media is powerful at raising a cause and weaker at delivering change.
