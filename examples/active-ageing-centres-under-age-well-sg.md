## Active Ageing Centres under Age Well SG

Under **Age Well SG**, Singapore significantly expanded its network of **Active Ageing Centres (AACs)** from 2024 to provide seniors with nearby spaces for exercise, social activities, volunteering and access to support services. The Government committed around **S$800 million from FY2024 to FY2028** to strengthen the centres, while the network grew from 154 centres at the launch of Age Well SG to **223 by March 2025**.

**Use:** This supports the argument that ageing policies should address not only seniors’ physical health but also loneliness and social isolation, enabling the elderly to remain active and socially connected members of society.
