## IMDA's AI Safety Red Teaming Challenge

In Singapore's IMDA AI Safety Red Teaming Challenge, testers identified more than 5,000 problematic responses from AI systems, including outputs that were culturally insensitive or offensive.

**Use:** This supports the argument that AI can reproduce biases embedded in its training data, so systematic testing and culturally specific safeguards are necessary before such systems are widely trusted.
