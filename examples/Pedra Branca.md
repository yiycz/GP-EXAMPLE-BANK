## Pedra Branca

In 2008, the International Court of Justice (ICJ) resolved a longstanding territorial dispute between Singapore and Malaysia, awarding sovereignty over Pedra Branca to Singapore and Middle Rocks to Malaysia, with both countries agreeing to abide by the legally binding judgment.

However, one must acknowledge the limitation of ICJ where its effectiveness lies in members' compliance. 

E.g. In March 2022, the ICJ ordered Russia to immediately suspend its military operations in Ukraine, yet Russia disregarded the legally binding provisional measures and continued its invasion.

**Use:** International judicial institutions can facilitate the peaceful resolution of territorial disputes by providing legally binding judgments that states respect, preventing disagreements over national sovereignty from escalating into armed conflicts.
