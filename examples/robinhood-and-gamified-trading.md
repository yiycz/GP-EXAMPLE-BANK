## Robinhood and Gamified Trading

The trading app Robinhood made buying stocks feel like a game, with confetti after each trade. Regulators fined it, saying it lured inexperienced young people into risky bets.

**Use:** This supports the argument that how a money platform is designed changes how people behave with money.
