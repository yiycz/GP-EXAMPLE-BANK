## Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)

Singapore’s **Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)** is the country’s main private-sector personal-data protection law. It regulates how organisations collect, use, disclose and protect personal information, imposing obligations involving areas such as notification, consent, purpose limitation, security, retention and data-breach notification.

**Use:** This supports the argument that technological and data-driven development can proceed alongside strong legal safeguards, allowing businesses to use personal data while protecting individuals against misuse and excessive intrusion.
