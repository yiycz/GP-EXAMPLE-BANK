## Liverpool F.C.’s Use of Data Analytics

Liverpool F.C. became well known for integrating data analytics into player recruitment and tactical planning under Jürgen Klopp. Its analysts used statistics such as expected goals, shot locations and chance creation alongside traditional scouting to identify players suited to the club's style of play. Mohamed Salah became one of Liverpool's most successful signings after the club's analytical approach helped identify his potential impact.

**Use:** This supports the argument that technology and data analytics can provide teams with a legitimate competitive advantage by improving recruitment and decision-making rather than directly replacing athletic ability.
