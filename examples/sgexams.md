## SGExams

Consider the felicitous example of the ‘SGExams’ community on Reddit, which serves as a hub for Singaporean students of all walks of life to engage in discourse with others like them regarding their studies. Especially during national examinations where stress runs rampant, students encourage and provide tips to each other in light of these adversities.


**Use:** Online communities encompass a wide range of topics, from gardening to extreme sports; any netizen can likely find a community that aligns with their interests.
