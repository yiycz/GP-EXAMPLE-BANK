## Cultural Appropriation in Fashion

Global fashion brands such as Zara have been accused of using Indigenous Mexican designs without permission or adequate recognition. Such controversies raise questions about who “owns” cultural artistic motifs and who should profit from them. They also expose unequal power dynamics between large Western corporations and marginalised communities whose cultural traditions may be commercialised without meaningful consultation or compensation.

**Use:** This supports the argument that artistic freedom and cultural exchange can become exploitative when powerful commercial actors profit from the cultural heritage of marginalised communities without adequately recognising or benefiting them.
