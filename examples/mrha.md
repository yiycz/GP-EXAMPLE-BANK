## Maintenance of Religious Harmony Act (MRHA)

Singapore’s **Maintenance of Religious Harmony Act (MRHA)**, introduced in 1990 and strengthened in 2019, allows the Government to take pre-emptive action against conduct that threatens religious harmony. Measures can include restraining orders and requirements to remove offensive online material. The law also seeks to preserve a separation between religion and politics and requires religious organisations to disclose certain foreign donations and affiliations.

**Use:** This supports the argument that governments may justifiably limit certain forms of religious or expressive freedom in order to preserve social harmony in a multi-religious society.
