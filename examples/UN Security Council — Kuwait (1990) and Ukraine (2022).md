## UN Security Council — Kuwait (1990) and Ukraine (2022)

In the early post-Cold War period, greater cooperation among major powers enabled the Security Council to act collectively, such as authorising action against Iraq after its 1990 invasion of Kuwait through Resolution 678 on 29 November 1990. The vote was 12-2-1, with both US and Soviet Union cooperating by voting for Security Council Resolution 678 to authorise force to restore international peace and security. By contrast, in 2022, amid renewed Russia–West strategic rivalry, Russia invaded Ukraine and then used its Security Council veto to block a resolution condemning its own actions, despite 11 members supporting it.

**Use:** In today’s more competitive environment, consensus dependence can sharply reduce an international organisation’s capacity to deliver decisive outcomes. Its relevance is called into question when powerful states can prevent the institution from performing its core functions.
