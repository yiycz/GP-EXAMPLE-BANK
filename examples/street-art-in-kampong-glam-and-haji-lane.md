## Street Art in Kampong Glam / Haji Lane

Street art in **Kampong Glam and Haji Lane** has become a distinctive part of the area’s cultural landscape. Murals and public artworks draw on local history, ethnic heritage and contemporary youth culture, helping to give the neighbourhood a recognisable visual identity while making art more accessible in everyday public spaces.

**Use:** This supports the argument that art can reinforce cultural identity by expressing the history, values and character of a community in ways that are visible and accessible to the public.
