## Lysenkoism in the Soviet Union

Under the Soviet Union, Trofim Lysenko's politically favoured but scientifically unsound ideas displaced modern genetics. Dissenting scientists were suppressed, and biological research was constrained by conformity to state ideology.

**Use:** This supports the argument that political self-interest can hinder science when authorities reward ideologically convenient claims and silence the scrutiny needed to correct false knowledge.
