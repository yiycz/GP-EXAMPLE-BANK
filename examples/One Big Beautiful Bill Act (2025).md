## Trump's One Big Beautiful Bill Act (2025)

On **4 July 2025**, US President **Donald Trump** signed the **One Big Beautiful Bill Act** into law, making major tax cuts permanent, introducing tax deductions for tips and overtime, increasing funding for border security and immigration enforcement, while reducing federal spending on **Medicaid and the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP)**. The **Congressional Budget Office (CBO)** projected that the legislation would **increase federal deficits by US$4.2 trillion between 2025 and 2034**, while its Medicaid provisions would leave an additional **7.5 million Americans uninsured by 2034**, highlighting the trade-offs between tax relief, fiscal sustainability and social welfare.

**Use:** Governments face competing pressures to stimulate economic growth, reduce taxation and safeguard social welfare, as policies intended to benefit certain groups may impose significant financial and social costs on others.
