## Facial Recognition and the End of Anonymity

Clearview AI scraped more than 30 billion public photographs from sites such as Facebook and Instagram to build a face-search tool for police. People had not consented to having their faces turned into searchable data. In 2024, Dutch regulators fined the company €30.5 million and declared the database illegal.

**Use:** This supports the argument that technological convenience and public safety can cost citizens their anonymity, because data-driven tools reduce people to searchable records and make freedom from surveillance difficult to preserve.
