## Government-run kindergartens

Pre-school education is largely run by the private sector, and so the quality of education varies widely from premier schools to those of a lesser quality. Those who can afford better quality education are far ahead of children who may not even have access to preschool due to affordability issues. 

MOE introduced government-run kindergartens to provide quality education to ensure pre-school education is given at affordable prices. 

The scheme has demonstrated encouraging progress, with the proportion of participating families achieving key income-security milestones rising from 26% in 2023 to 28% in 2024, although the modest improvement suggests that breaking intergenerational poverty remains a long-term challenge. 

**Use:** Governments should ensure the provision of public infrastructures, amenities and services.
