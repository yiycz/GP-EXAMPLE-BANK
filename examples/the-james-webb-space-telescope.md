## The James Webb Space Telescope

NASA's James Webb Space Telescope launched in late 2021 and released its first images in July 2022. One showed thousands of galaxies in a tiny patch of sky, using light that had travelled for more than 13 billion years. The telescope cost about US$10 billion and has revealed galaxies and details no previous instrument could see.

**Use:** This supports the argument that science enriches humanity by expanding knowledge and wonder, not merely by solving immediate practical problems, although its enormous cost raises questions about competing needs on Earth.
