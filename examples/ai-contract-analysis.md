## JPMorgan’s AI Contract Analysis

JPMorgan has used machine learning to analyse legal agreements and commercial loan contracts through its COiN system. Work that previously required lawyers and loan officers around **360,000 hours annually** could be completed by the system in seconds, allowing large volumes of documents to be processed far more efficiently.

**Use:** This supports the argument that AI can dramatically improve productivity by automating repetitive and time-consuming professional tasks, allowing skilled workers to devote more attention to work requiring human judgement and expertise.
