## Solar Deployment under the Singapore Green Plan 2030

Under the **Singapore Green Plan 2030**, Singapore aims to deploy **at least 2 gigawatt-peak (GWp) of solar energy by 2030**, enough to meet around **3% of projected electricity demand** and power roughly **350,000 households annually**. Solar panels are being installed on HDB rooftops, reservoirs and other available spaces, while energy-storage systems help manage the intermittency of solar power.

**Use:** This supports the argument that even land-scarce countries can expand renewable energy by using urban infrastructure creatively and combining clean-energy generation with supporting technologies such as energy storage.
