## Jacinda Ardern — Christchurch Mosque Attacks (2019)

Following the Christchurch mosque attacks in 2019, Prime Minister Jacinda Ardern responded swiftly by communicating regularly with the public, supporting affected communities, and introducing tighter gun-control measures. Her government's actions were widely covered and scrutinised online, leading to many citizens viewing the response as compassionate, decisive, and transparent. The government's visible leadership and clear communication in the digital age strengthened public confidence, showing that effective governance can reinforce trust even during times of national crisis.

**Use:** In today's highly connected and information-rich society, many governments have demonstrated strong competence, decisiveness, and transparency, particularly during periods of crisis. This consistent and visible performance allows public trust to be sustained and even strengthened rather than eroded.
