## Catching a Rocket From the Sky

In October 2024, SpaceX caught its returning 70-metre Starship booster in mid-air with giant mechanical arms nicknamed 'chopsticks'. Reusing rockets could sharply reduce the cost of space travel, and SpaceX says Starship may one day carry people to Mars. Mars settlement remains distant and unproven.

**Use:** This supports the argument that private ambition can accelerate scientific and engineering progress, while raising questions about who should control the frontier and whether spectacular advances justify distant promises.
