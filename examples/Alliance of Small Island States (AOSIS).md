## Alliance of Small Island States (AOSIS)

The Alliance of Small Island States (AOSIS) is a coalition of 39 small island and low-lying coastal developing states, established in 1990 to collectively advocate for their interests in international climate negotiations. It includes Maldives and Antigua and Barbuda

AOSIS, representing vulnerable small island states, championed the slogan “1.5°C to Stay Alive”, successfully advocating for the Paris Agreement to include the goal of pursuing efforts to limit global warming to 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels.

**Use:** Smaller states can pool their diplomatic weight, frame climate change as as issue of justice and survival, and push larger emitters towards stronger commitments.
