## Video Calls On Social Media Apps

One can instantly share what they are currently doing with friends daily with a click of a button. The advent of video calls on apps like WhatsApp has also enabled individuals to stay in touch with loved ones regardless of location. In other words, social media has brought about a new era in which our connections with others transcend beyond geographical boundaries, resulting in the sustaining and strengthening of human relationships.


**Use:** There is a certain degree of veracity in the viewpoint that social media does bolster our connections with others.
