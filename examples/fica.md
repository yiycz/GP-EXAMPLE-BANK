## Foreign Interference (Countermeasures) Act (FICA)

Singapore introduced the **Foreign Interference (Countermeasures) Act (FICA) in 2021** to detect and counter covert foreign interference in domestic politics, including hostile information campaigns conducted through social media and local proxies. Authorities can issue directions to online platforms and services to obtain information or restrict communications linked to foreign interference.

**Use:** This supports the argument that governments may restrict some political and informational freedoms to protect national sovereignty, especially when digital technologies make covert foreign influence easier to conduct and harder to detect.
