## Russia's Invasion of Ukraine (2022)

On **24 February 2022**, Russia launched a **full-scale military invasion of Ukraine**, violating its territorial sovereignty and the **UN Charter's prohibition against the use of force against another state's territorial integrity**, without authorisation from the UN Security Council or a recognised legal justification of self-defence. On **2 March 2022**, the **UN General Assembly adopted Resolution ES-11/1 with 141 votes in favour**, deploring Russia's aggression and demanding the withdrawal of its forces, demonstrating a violation of **jus ad bellum** despite Russia's attempts to justify its military intervention.

**Use:** Powerful states may disregard international legal restrictions when pursuing strategic interests, exposing the limitations of international organisations in preventing unlawful military aggression.
