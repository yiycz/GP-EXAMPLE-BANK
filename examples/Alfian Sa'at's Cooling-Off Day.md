## Alfian Sa'at's Cooling-Off Day

Alfian Sa'at's Cooling-Off Day captures Singapore's 2011 General Election, presents a variety of viewpoints and gives particular attention to opposition voices. It portrays Singaporean society as unwilling to confront some uncomfortable realities inherent in a democratic society.

**Use:** The arts serve as a powerful form of social commentary that highlights social issues and raises public awareness of them. -> safe space to examine social issues without confrontations, help to facilitate sensitive issues and thus build cross-cultural understanding over time.
