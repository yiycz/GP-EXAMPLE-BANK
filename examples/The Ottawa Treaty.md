## The Ottawa Treaty

The Ottawa treaty gained traction as changing humanitarian norms redefine landmines as illegitimate weapons

As the international community increasingly recognises the indiscriminate humanitarian harm caused by landmines, their use becomes morally unacceptable. This creates international pressure on states to abandon such weapons and adopt common restrictions, even when doing so may limit their military options.


**Use:** International cooperation can be driven by shared moral values and evolving humanitarian norms, rather than merely national self-interest.
