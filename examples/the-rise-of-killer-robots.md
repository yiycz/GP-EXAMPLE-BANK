## The Rise of Killer Robots

Autonomous weapons can select and kill targets without a human pressing the trigger, and drones in Ukraine have been reported to strike without direct human control. In December 2023, 152 countries backed the first United Nations resolution warning about such 'killer robots'. No binding treaty exists, and powers such as Russia resist limits.

**Use:** This supports the argument that autonomous weapons remove human conscience from killing and create an accountability gap; because no state wants to fall behind, only shared international rules can restrain the arms race.
