## Harper Lee's To Kill a Mockingbird

Harper Lee's To Kill a Mockingbird deals primarily with racism in the American South and exposes deep-seated prejudice against the Black community. It was banned from numerous school libraries because of its controversial portrayal of extreme racism and repeated use of racial slurs.

**Use:** This supports the argument that literature can expose deep-rooted social prejudice and promote justice, even though its uncomfortable depiction of racism may provoke demands for censorship.
