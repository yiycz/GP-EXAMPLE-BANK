## Lance Armstrong Doping Scandal

Lance Armstrong won seven consecutive editions of the Tour de France before investigations uncovered evidence of systematic doping, including the use of erythropoietin (EPO), which increases red blood cell production and can enhance endurance. His Tour de France titles were subsequently stripped, and the scandal severely damaged his reputation and public trust in professional cycling.

**Use:** This supports the argument that performance-enhancing drugs undermine the integrity of sport because they provide artificial advantages, violate fair competition and erode public trust in athletic achievement.
