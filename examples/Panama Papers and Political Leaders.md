## Panama Papers and Political Leaders

Several prominent world leaders and politicians were named in the Panama Papers leak published by the International Consortium of Investigative Journalists (ICIJ). They include: The former Prime Minister of Pakistan who faced intense pressure and was eventually disqualified from office after his family's offshore shell companies were exposed; Sigmundur Davíð Gunnlaugsson, the former Prime Minister of Iceland resigned after the leak revealed he and his wife co-owned an undisclosed offshore company holding millions in bonds; and Ilham Aliyev, the President of Azerbaijan who was linked to an empire of secret offshore companies used to hold luxury assets and properties.

**Use:** Even the most accomplished and respected politicians today have their secret flaws and sins, some of which can be fatal for their reputation once revealed. With the dogged inquisitiveness of the media today, all too often they will be found out and end up being outed as villains as their final legacy, their contributions forgotten by a vindictive public who expect their leaders to be perfect.
