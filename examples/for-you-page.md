## for you page

The ‘for you page’ on Tiktok and the Youtube feed both of which are programmed to recommend a user content based on his previously liked or saved videos. It may at first seem inconsequential. However, this has far-reaching implications for society, when social media sites like Twitter display news based on an individual’s political stance, leading to the increasing divide between liberals and conservatives.

**Use:**  Social media ultimately promotes disunity more than it unites, due to the development of ‘cancel culture’, as a result of the conformist culture engendered by social media. Due to the nature of social media, an individual is often subjected to limited content, as social media algorithms only recommend users’ content that serves to reinforce their existing beliefs, interest, and biases.
