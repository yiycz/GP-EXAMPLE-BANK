## Stronger Social-Media Safeguards for Youths

At the **2026 National Day Rally**, Singapore announced stronger safeguards for young social-media users, including more robust age-verification measures instead of relying solely on self-declared ages and stronger safety-by-design requirements for platforms. The Government also indicated that it could consider raising the minimum access age above 13 for platforms whose protections remain inadequate.

**Use:** This supports the argument that governments can place greater responsibility on technology companies to protect vulnerable users, even if doing so restricts platform access or individual choice in the interests of youth welfare.
