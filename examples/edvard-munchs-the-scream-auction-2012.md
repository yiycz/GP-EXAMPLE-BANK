## Edvard Munch's The Scream auction (2012)

Edvard Munch's The Scream commanded US$120 million at a Sotheby's auction in 2012 after an opening bid of US$40 million.

**Use:** This supports the argument that artworks can function as valuable investment assets because their scarcity and cultural importance may cause their market value to rise significantly over time.
