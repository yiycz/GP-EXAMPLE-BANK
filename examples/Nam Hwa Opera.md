## Nam Hwa Opera

Nam Hwa Opera preserves traditional Teochew opera through performances, workshops and trainings. 
Who Says It First：A modern Mandopop musical incorporating Teochew opera elements, exploring the gradual disappearance of dialect culture among younger Singaporeans and the struggles of people living with dementia. It attracted 864 audience members. 
It also established the Yeo Khee Lim Teochew culture research centre to document cultural heritage. 

**Use:** The arts can be used to preserve cultural heritage as they themselves are powerful embodiments of cultures, traditions and values.
