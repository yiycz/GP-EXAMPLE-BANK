## SG Climate Rally

**SG Climate Rally** has organised public events and educational campaigns to raise awareness of climate change and encourage discussion of Singapore’s environmental policies. Local environmental groups have also called for faster action in areas such as waste reduction, biodiversity protection and climate mitigation, arguing that the urgency of climate change requires stronger government action.

**Use:** This supports the argument that environmental activism can strengthen public awareness and place pressure on governments to accelerate climate action, even when activists themselves do not possess formal policymaking power.
