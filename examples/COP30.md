## COP30 

At COP30 in Brazil in 2025, countries agreed to call for **tripling adaptation finance** by 2035 and adopted **indicators to monitor climate adaptation**, but **failed** to reach consensus on a fossil-fuel transition roadmap.

Note that adaptation finance is "funding explicitly intended to help communities and economies reduce physical climate risks, build resilience, and adjust to actual or anticipated climate effects like extreme weather, floods, and sea-level rise." 

**Use:**  International organisations are often bogged down by the lowest common denominator problem, whereby conflicting national interests force member states to settle for watered-down agreements that undermine the effectiveness of international cooperation.
