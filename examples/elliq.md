## ElliQ AI Companion for Older Adults

**ElliQ** is an AI-powered social companion designed for older adults who live alone or experience loneliness. It can hold conversations, suggest activities, provide health reminders, offer cognitive games and facilitate video calls. New York State distributed ElliQ devices to hundreds of older adults, with research suggesting that such systems can improve engagement and reduce feelings of loneliness.

**Use:** This supports the argument that AI can help alleviate elderly loneliness and complement human care, but excessive reliance on artificial companionship may mask social isolation if governments or families begin treating technology as a substitute for genuine human relationships.
