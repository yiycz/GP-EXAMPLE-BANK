## Trump's America First Policy (2025–2026)

Since returning to office in **January 2025**, President Trump has pursued an **“America First” policy** prioritising US economic competitiveness and national sovereignty over certain multilateral commitments, announcing a **10% baseline tariff on most imports in April 2025**, alongside higher tariffs on selected trading partners, and initiating the withdrawal of the US from the **Paris Climate Agreement**, which took effect on **27 January 2026**. His administration also announced the termination of **83% of USAID programmes in March 2025**, while the sweeping tariff announcements triggered financial-market turmoil and international trade tensions, illustrating how the pursuit of domestic national interests can generate significant consequences for **international cooperation, global trade and humanitarian assistance**.

**Use:** Governments may prioritise national interests over global responsibilities to protect domestic economic and political interests, even when such decisions undermine multilateral cooperation and impose costs on other countries.
