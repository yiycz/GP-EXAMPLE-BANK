## Arther Yap's 2 Mothers in a HDB background

 Arther Yap's 2 Mothers in a HDB background humorously satirized the kind of comparison that Singaporean parents make between their children. This poem has a strong Singaporean flavour and employs similar symbols of comparisons such grades that are immediately recognizable to Singaporeans. 

**Use:** The arts are often unique to a culture, precisely as they bear values, attitudes and creativity that comes with the people in that culture.
