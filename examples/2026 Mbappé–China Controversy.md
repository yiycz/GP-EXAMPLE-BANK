## 2026 Mbappé–China Controversy

In October 2026, French footballer Kylian Mbappé faced backlash in China after appearing to describe Mao Zedong as a dictator, prompting three Chinese companies to terminate sponsorship deals and Chinese state television to suspend La Liga broadcasts, after which Mbappé issued a public apology on 27 October.
