## Hollywood's Writers Strike Over AI

In 2023, Hollywood writers struck for 148 days and won the first contract rules stopping studios from replacing them with AI. Studios cannot use AI to write or rewrite scripts, or to reduce a writer's credit or pay. Writers may choose to use AI but cannot be forced to, although studios may still train AI on past scripts.

**Use:** This supports the argument that human creators remain valuable and that AI replacement is not technologically inevitable: law, collective bargaining and institutional choices determine how the tool is used.
