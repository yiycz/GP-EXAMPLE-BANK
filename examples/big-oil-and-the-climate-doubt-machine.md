## Big Oil and the Climate Doubt Machine

ExxonMobil conducted serious climate research in the 1970s and 1980s, and a 2023 study found that its scientists had predicted global warming with remarkable accuracy. Yet the company publicly funded doubt and portrayed climate science as uncertain, delaying action that could threaten fossil-fuel profits.

**Use:** This supports the argument that selfish corporate interests can limit scientific progress not by preventing discovery but by hiding, denying and confusing reliable knowledge that conflicts with profit.
