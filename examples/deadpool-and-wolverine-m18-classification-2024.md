## Deadpool & Wolverine M18 classification (2024)

Singapore's IMDA classified the 2024 film Deadpool & Wolverine as M18 because of its strong violence and coarse language.

**Use:** This supports the argument that age-based media regulation can protect younger audiences from content considered unsuitable for their level of maturity without banning it for adults.
