## Operation Car Wash (Lava Jato) — Brazil

Operation Car Wash (Lava Jato) was a major anti-corruption investigation that uncovered a vast bribery network involving politicians, business leaders, and executives at the Brazilian state-owned oil company Petrobras. Construction companies were found to have paid bribes to Petrobras executives and politicians in exchange for lucrative government contracts amounting to billions. Every new revelation was instantly reported by online news outlets and widely shared on social media platforms from 2014-2021. Because digital media continuously circulated information about the scandal, each new disclosure reinforced the perception that corruption was widespread among political elites. This caused public trust in Brazil's government and political institutions to decline significantly, demonstrating how scandals today spread faster, reach larger audiences, and have a more lasting impact on public confidence.

**Use:** In today's digital age, political scandals are exposed faster, spread more widely, and stay in the public eye for longer, causing each new scandal to further increase public distrust rather than being forgotten as a separate incident.
