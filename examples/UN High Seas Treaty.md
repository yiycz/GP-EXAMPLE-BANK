## UN High Seas Treaty

The UN High Seas Treaty entered into force on 17 January 2026 after securing the required 60 ratifications, establishing legally binding rules for protecting marine biodiversity in international waters. -> International cooperation can overcome the limitations of national jurisdictions by establishing common legal frameworks to protect shared environmental resources.

**Use:** International organisations facilitate collective environmental action by coordinating states' resources and establishing common rules to address transnational environmental challenges that individual nations cannot effectively tackle alone.
