## The Golden Staircase

In March 2017, Singaporean artist Priyageetha Dia, then a LASALLE student, covered a staircase on the 20th floor of Block 103 Jalan Rajah with gold foil as an artistic intervention.
- Artistic intention: She wanted to transform an ordinary public space into an artwork while paying homage to her family's goldsmithing heritage.
- Controversy: Jalan Besar Town Council declared the installation unauthorised under its by-laws governing common property.
- Outcome: Dia voluntarily removed the gold foil on 12 March 2017, following a nationwide debate over whether her work constituted art or vandalism.
