## Colombia–FARC Peace Agreement (2016)

On **24 November 2016**, the Colombian government and the **Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia (FARC)** signed a peace agreement intended to end more than **five decades of armed conflict**, establishing provisions for **disarmament, reintegration of former combatants, transitional justice and political participation**. Under UN supervision, the FARC completed the laying down of **8,994 weapons by September 2017**, while the UN Verification Mission continued monitoring peace implementation, demonstrating how **jus post bellum principles of reconciliation, accountability and institutional reconstruction** can help transform military conflict into a more sustainable political settlement.

**Use:** Lasting peace requires more than ending military hostilities, as post-war reconciliation, accountability and the reintegration of former combatants are essential to rebuilding legitimate institutions and preventing renewed violence.
