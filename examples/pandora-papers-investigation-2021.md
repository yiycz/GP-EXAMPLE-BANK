## Pandora Papers investigation (2021)

In 2021, the International Consortium of Investigative Journalists released the Pandora Papers, nearly 12 million leaked documents revealing the hidden and sometimes unethical dealings of wealthy and powerful figures, including prominent world leaders and celebrities.

**Use:** This supports the argument that independent investigative journalism holds powerful individuals accountable by exposing conduct that would otherwise remain concealed.
