## Watergate investigation

The Washington Post's investigation into the 1972 Watergate scandal revealed that the burglars who broke into the Democratic Party's headquarters had links to President Nixon's re-election campaign, contributing to Nixon's resignation in 1974.

**Use:** This supports the argument that a free press strengthens democracy by investigating government misconduct and preventing leaders from monopolising power and information.
