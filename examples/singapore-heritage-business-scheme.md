## Singapore Heritage Business Scheme

Singapore’s **Heritage Business Scheme** provides branding, marketing and business-development support to longstanding local enterprises with strong cultural value. Heritage businesses such as **Bengawan Solo** have benefited from such support while building strong commercial recognition, showing that traditional businesses can preserve cultural identity while remaining economically successful.

**Use:** This supports the argument that governments can help preserve cultural heritage without sacrificing commercial viability, as targeted support enables traditional businesses to modernise, expand and remain relevant to contemporary consumers.
