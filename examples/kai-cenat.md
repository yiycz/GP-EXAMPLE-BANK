## Kai Cenat

A case in point is Kai Cenat, who has faced criticism for reacting light-heartedly to memes containing racist and sexist undertones during his livestreams. In one instance, he laughed at a racist meme and remarked that “Black people have no culture,” while he was also criticised for streaming inside a prison and portraying the experience in an entertaining manner. By treating such sensitive issues as comedy, he risks trivialising discrimination and encouraging his large audience to view these matters as insignificant.



**Use:** The idolisation of undeserving media influencers breeds harmful behaviour and perceptions, warping our social fabric. Influencers become problematic when they bring their behaviour from the virtual world- where they can act with near impunity- to the real world, poking fun at sensitive, real issues, threatening to spark chaos.
