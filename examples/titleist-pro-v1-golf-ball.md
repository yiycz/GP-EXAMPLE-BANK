## Titleist Pro V1 Golf Ball

Introduced in 2000, the Titleist Pro V1 became one of the most influential technological innovations in modern golf. Many professional golfers quickly adopted it because its design allowed the ball to travel farther while retaining the accuracy, control and feel demanded by elite players.

**Use:** This supports the argument that technological innovation can raise sporting performance by improving equipment, although it also raises questions about how much competitive advantage should come from technology rather than the athlete.
