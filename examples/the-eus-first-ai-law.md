## The EU's First AI Law

In 2024, the European Union passed the world's first broad AI law. The AI Act bans uses such as scoring citizens by behaviour, subjects high-risk systems in areas such as hiring and policing to strict checks, and allows fines of up to 7% of a company's worldwide revenue. It does not cover military AI.

**Use:** This supports the argument that enforceable AI regulation is both possible and necessary, although strict rules may slow innovation and remain limited by jurisdictional and military gaps.
