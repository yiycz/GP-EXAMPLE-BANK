## INTERPOL and AFRIPOL's Operation Catalyst (2025)

Between July and September 2025, INTERPOL and AFRIPOL coordinated operations across six African countries, resulting in 83 arrests, including 21 for terrorism-related crimes, while identifying approximately US$260 million potentially linked to terrorist financing.

**Use:** International organisations facilitate intelligence sharing and coordinated law enforcement to disrupt the financial networks sustaining terrorist organisations.
