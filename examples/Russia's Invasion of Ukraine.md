## Russia's Invasion of Ukraine

Since Russia is not a party to the Rome Statute, the ICC lacks jurisdiction to prosecute its leaders for the crime of aggression against Ukraine, prompting an international coalition to establish a separate tribunal through an agreement signed in June 2025.

**Use:** International judicial institutions are constrained by their limited jurisdiction, as states that refuse to accept certain international legal obligations can impede efforts to hold their leaders accountable.
