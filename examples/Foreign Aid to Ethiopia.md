## Foreign Aid to Ethiopia

As of March 2026, a **World Bank-administered programme supported by Canada** and other foreign donors had provided **food and cash transfers** to over 8.5 million Ethiopians, while helping nearly 794,000 people receive **financial literacy** and **business training** and **supporting the development of roads**, **irrigation systems** and **other infrastructure.** 
Simultaneously developing human capital and productive infrastructure, enabling recipient countries to strengthen their long-term economic capacity and reduce dependence on external assistance.

**Use:** Foreign aid can alleviate immediate financial hardship, acting as a stop-gap measure to help countries in the short run, and nations can build up their capacity achievement developments.
