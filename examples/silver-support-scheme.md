## Silver Support Scheme

Singapore’s **Silver Support Scheme**, introduced in 2016 and enhanced in 2025, provides quarterly cash supplements to lower-income citizens aged 65 and above who had relatively low lifetime earnings and limited household resources. Payments were increased in 2025, with the enhanced scheme supporting close to **290,000 seniors**. Eligible recipients receive support automatically without having to apply.

**Use:** This supports the argument that governments can protect financially vulnerable elderly people through targeted redistribution, while retaining a means-tested welfare system that directs greater assistance towards those with the greatest need.
