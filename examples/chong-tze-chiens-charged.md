## Chong Tze Chien's Charged

Charged is a critically acclaimed stage play by Chong Tze Chien that depicts racial prejudice within National Service and the emotional burden carried by minority Singaporeans, sparking discussion about casual racism.

**Use:** This supports the argument that theatre can expose subtle, deep-rooted prejudice and initiate necessary conversations about racial experiences that are often overlooked.
