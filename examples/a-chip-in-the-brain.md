## A Chip in the Brain

In January 2024, Neuralink implanted its first brain chip in a human, Noland Arbaugh, who is paralysed from the shoulders down. The coin-sized device reads signals from more than 1,000 electrodes and converts intended movement into cursor control. Arbaugh can use a computer, play chess and play games by thought alone for up to ten hours a day.

**Use:** This supports the argument that merging mind and machine can restore independence lost through illness, while raising profound questions about safety, control and what it means to remain human.
