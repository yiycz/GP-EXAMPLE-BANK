## Rootport Midjourney

Japanese author Rootport was a talented writer who lacked the drawing skills necessary to fulfill his dream of creating a comic. By turning to the artificial intelligence software Midjourney, he generated an entire manga in just six weeks—a feat that would take a skilled human illustrator years to complete.

**Use:** This demonstrates how technology successfully intervenes to bridge our skill gaps, empowering individuals to finalize artwork they could never independently complete.
