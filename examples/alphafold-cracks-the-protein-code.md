## AlphaFold Cracks the Protein Code

In 2024, the Nobel Prize in Chemistry went to the makers of AlphaFold, an AI that predicts a protein's three-dimensional shape from its code in minutes. It has modelled nearly all 200 million proteins known to science, and the results are freely available to more than two million researchers. Scientists must still test and verify its predictions in the laboratory.

**Use:** This supports the argument that AI can multiply human effort and serve the public good by solving difficult scientific problems, but it remains a tool whose answers require human testing and verification.
