## Human Biomedical Research Act (HBRA)

Singapore’s **Human Biomedical Research Act (2015)** regulates human biomedical research and the handling of human tissue to ensure that the health, welfare and safety of research participants remain paramount. Researchers and institutions must comply with ethical and scientific requirements designed to protect research subjects and tissue donors.

**Use:** This supports the argument that scientific advancement cannot justify disregarding ethical concerns, as the welfare and rights of human participants must remain central even when research could produce valuable discoveries.
