## Paris AI Action Summit (2025)

At the 2025 Paris AI Action Summit, world leaders raised concerns that the rapid expansion of artificial intelligence could undermine environmental sustainability by increasing electricity consumption, carbon emissions and demand for natural resources. 

France, UNEP and the International Telecommunication Union launched a coalition of 91 partners, including technology companies and international organisations, to promote energy-efficient AI development.

At the 2025 Paris AI Action Summit, 65 countries and regional bodies endorsed a declaration promoting international AI governance, but the US and UK declined to sign amid American concerns over excessive regulation stifling innovation and British concerns over inadequate national security safeguards. -> countries fear that collective regulations may compromise their economic competitiveness and national security.
