## Andres Serrano’s Piss Christ

Andres Serrano’s 1987 photograph *Piss Christ* depicts a crucifix submerged in the artist’s urine. Many Christians regarded the work as blasphemous and offensive because it appeared to degrade a central religious symbol. Defenders, however, argued that the artwork could be interpreted as a critique of the commercialisation and treatment of religious symbols.

**Use:** This supports the argument that offensive art may still possess artistic or social value, as provocation can be deliberately used to challenge audiences and encourage reflection on how society treats sacred symbols.
