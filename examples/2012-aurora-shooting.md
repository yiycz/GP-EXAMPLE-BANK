## 2012 Aurora shooting

The 2012 Aurora shooting occurred **during a screening of The Dark Knight Rises** and was widely discussed in relation to **alleged inspiration** from the Batman franchise.

**Use:** This can support the argument that people may **imitate violent behaviour** seen on television and the Internet, and that graphic or gory entertainment may be blamed for normalising violence. However, it shows public concern about **copycat effects** rather than proving direct causation.
