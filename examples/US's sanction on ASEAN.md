## US's sanction on ASEAN

Western major countries such as the US placed economic sanctions on ASEAN for permitting **Myanmar**'s entry in 1997 amidst the **SLORC's abysmal human rights abuse** of the **Rohingya**. This proves the fact that the multifaceted nature of IR does not solely function on thre premise of self-motivated interests. 

**Use:** International cooperation can sometimes be genuine and sincere in the area of defending universal human rights.
