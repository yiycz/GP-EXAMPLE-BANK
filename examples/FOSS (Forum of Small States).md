## FOSS (Forum of Small States)

FOSS (Forum of Small States) is an informal grouping of small countries within the United Nations, co-founded by Singapore in 1992 to promote cooperation and amplify the voices of smaller states in international affairs. 

The bargaining power of FOSS allowed small states like Ireland to secure enough votes to win one of the on-permanent seat on the UNSC.

**Use:** Smaller countries can exponentially increase their bargaining power and strategic deposition by forming blocs with other small nations.
