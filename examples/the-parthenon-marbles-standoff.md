## The Parthenon Marbles Standoff

In 2023, a row over the Parthenon Marbles sank a UK-Greece meeting. Greece wants the 2,500-year-old sculptures back; Britain has held them for 200 years.

**Use:** This supports the argument that heritage is about belonging as much as preservation. Where an object sits is a claim about who it belongs to.
