## Liu Kang's Life by the River

Liu Kang's Life by the River depicts traditional kampong life in 1970s Singapore, featuring wooden stilt houses and villagers engaging in everyday activities along a river. -> essential historical docuements of the early scenes of SG as a colonial outpost. 

**Use:** The arts are often unique to a culture, precisely as they bear values, attitudes and creativity that comes with the people in that culture.
