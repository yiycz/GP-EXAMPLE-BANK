## 2025 Singapore's Humanitarian Assistance to Gaza

In December 2025, **Singapore** pledged another **S$1 million** towards humanitarian relief in Gaza, bringing its total assistance since October 2023 to over **S$25 million**, **despite maintaining diplomatic relations with Israel**.

**Use:** A state's genuine commitment to humanitarian responsibilities can cultivate international **goodwill and credibility**, which serve as valuable sources of soft power.
