## Xinyao Music

Xinyao music is original Mandarin songs that are composed by Singaporeans. It is an important cultural heritage as Xinyao gives young Singaporeans first authentic musical identity, instead of solely relying on imported pop Mandarin songs. 

The lyrics served as poetic snapshots of growing up in a rapidly modernizing city-state, capturing themes of school life, teenage romance, national belonging, and the unique social psyche of Singaporean youths.

**Use:** The arts can be used to preserve cultural heritage as they themselves are powerful embodiments of cultures, traditions and values.
