## Money Guys Show

The Money Guys Show on YouTube helps individuals build investing confidence and debunks online financial myths, garnering 140 million views. For younger audiences, YouTuber Nika Erculj has amassed half a million subscribers by teaching basic budgeting and saving. Additionally, economic news outlets like the Financial Times keep users informed about the unfolding global financial situation.
