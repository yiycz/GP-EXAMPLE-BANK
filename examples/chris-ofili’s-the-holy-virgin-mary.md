## Chris Ofili’s The Holy Virgin Mary

Chris Ofili’s 1996 artwork *The Holy Virgin Mary* depicted the Virgin Mary using unconventional materials, including elephant dung and pornographic imagery. The work offended some Christians, who regarded its treatment of a sacred religious figure as disrespectful and blasphemous. It became especially controversial when it was exhibited in New York in 1999.

**Use:** This supports the argument that provocative art can cause genuine offence when it challenges deeply held religious beliefs, raising the question of whether artistic freedom should be limited by respect for sacred values.
