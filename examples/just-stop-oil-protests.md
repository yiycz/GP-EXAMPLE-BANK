## Just Stop Oil Protests

Just Stop Oil protesters have used disruptive tactics such as blocking major roads, interrupting sporting events and throwing soup at the protective glass covering Vincent van Gogh’s *Sunflowers*. These actions generated substantial media attention, but they were also criticised for disrupting daily life and alienating members of the public. Critics further argued that such protests did not directly reduce emissions or produce major changes in UK climate policy.

**Use:** This supports the argument that provocative activism can attract attention to important causes, but excessive disruption may undermine public support and fail to translate visibility into meaningful policy change.
