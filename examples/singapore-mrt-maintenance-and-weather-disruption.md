## Singapore MRT Maintenance and Weather Disruption

Singapore's rail operators conduct regular overnight maintenance to inspect tracks and equipment, reducing avoidable failures. Yet a 2024 MRT disruption attributed to a lightning strike still suspended service for about two hours, showing that preventive work cannot remove every external risk.

**Use:** This supports a balanced argument about public transport: careful maintenance can make a network dependable, but even a well-managed system remains vulnerable to exceptional weather and other unforeseen events.
