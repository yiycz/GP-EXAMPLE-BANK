## Protection from Online Falsehoods and Manipulation Act (POFMA)

Singapore introduced the **Protection from Online Falsehoods and Manipulation Act (POFMA) in 2019** to address false statements of fact communicated online. Where intervention is considered to be in the public interest, the Government may issue a **Correction Direction**, which generally requires the original post to remain accessible but to display a notice linking readers to the Government’s clarification. More serious cases may attract stop-communication or disabling directions.

**Use:** This supports the argument that governments may regulate online misinformation to protect the public from harmful falsehoods, but such powers also raise concerns about whether state intervention can constrain freedom of expression.
