## Singapore’s Online Safety Code

Singapore introduced a legally binding **Online Safety Code in 2023** requiring designated major social-media services to implement measures against harmful online content. The Code covers areas such as sexual and violent material, cyberbullying, suicide and self-harm content, and material facilitating vice or organised crime, with particular emphasis on protecting children.

**Use:** This supports the argument that governments can place greater responsibility on technology platforms to reduce online harms, rather than relying solely on individual users to protect themselves.
