## UN Security Council — Gaza Ceasefire Delays (2023–2024)

After the outbreak of the Gaza war in October 2023, the UN Security Council took months to agree on meaningful action because permanent members disagreed over the wording of ceasefire resolutions. Although ceasefire proposals were debated within weeks, vetoes blocked several resolutions, and the Council adopted its first resolution demanding an immediate ceasefire only in March 2024, 171 days into the war. This illustrates how an IO dependent on agreement among its members can respond too slowly to a rapidly worsening crisis, reducing its effectiveness when timely intervention is most needed.

Use: Both examples show that international organisations may be slow for different structural reasons – bureaucracy in one case and political deadlock in the other – but the result is similar, that global crises today can escalate faster than international organisations can produce decisive collective action, thereby contributing to the view of international organisations not being relevant today.
