## Mikayla Nogueira Criticise

Skincare influencer Mikayla Nogueira with 17 million followers was criticised for her comment in 2020 on how hard her job was, “Just finished working, it’s 5:19– try being an influencer for a day.” Many of her followers, especially those who work laborious jobs criticised her for being insensitive and entitled, proving that even influencers with a high degree of influential power do not get to abuse their fame by spitting out their views with impunity.


**Use:**  The people who consume the media often will not hesitate to lambast them should the influencers behave in an entitled manner and share their “first-world problems”.
