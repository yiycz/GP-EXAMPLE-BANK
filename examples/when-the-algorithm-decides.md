## When the Algorithm Decides

When UK examinations were cancelled in 2020, an algorithm used schools' past performance to determine A-level grades. Almost 40% of grades were lower than teachers had predicted, and capable students at weaker schools were punished; public outrage forced a reversal within days. A similar Dutch data system wrongly labelled thousands of families as benefit fraudsters.

**Use:** This supports the argument that data should inform decisions rather than make them alone: a formula can appear objective while ignoring individual effort, fairness and the person behind the numbers.
