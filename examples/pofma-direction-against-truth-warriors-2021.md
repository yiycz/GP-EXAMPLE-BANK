## POFMA direction against Truth Warriors (2021)

In 2021, Singapore invoked POFMA against the Truth Warriors website for spreading false claims about COVID-19 vaccines and ivermectin, requiring correction notices to counter the misinformation.

**Use:** This supports the argument that regulation of online falsehoods can protect public health by ensuring that readers are exposed to official corrections alongside harmful misinformation.
