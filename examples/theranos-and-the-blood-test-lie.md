## Theranos and the Blood-Test Lie

Theranos claimed that its machine could run hundreds of tests from a single drop of blood, but the technology did not work and produced unreliable results that risked patients' health. The company fabricated patient records and used traditional third-party machines instead. Founder Elizabeth Holmes was sentenced to more than 11 years in prison in 2022.

**Use:** This supports the argument that science is only as reliable as its evidence: confidence, prestige and funding cannot replace proof, and selfish commercial agendas can cause technology to promise far more than it delivers. It also shows how investigative journalism can expose such deception.
