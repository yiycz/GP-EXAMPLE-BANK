## BTS at the United Nations

In 2021, South Korean K-pop group BTS was appointed as special presidential envoys and addressed the United Nations General Assembly's SDG Moment, promoting youth empowerment, climate awareness and international cooperation.

**Use:** By leveraging BTS's immense global popularity to promote universal values, South Korea strengthened its international appeal and favourable national image, thereby expanding its soft power and diplomatic influence.
