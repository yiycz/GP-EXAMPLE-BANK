## Elgin Marbles / Parthenon Sculptures Dispute

The Elgin Marbles, also known as the Parthenon Sculptures, have been at the centre of a long-running dispute between Greece and the United Kingdom. Removed from the Parthenon in the early 1800s, the sculptures are now held by the British Museum. Greece argues that their removal was unethical and that they should be returned as part of the country’s cultural heritage, while the British Museum maintains that they were legally acquired and are accessible to millions of visitors each year.

**Use:** This supports the argument that museums face an ethical tension between preserving and displaying cultural artefacts for a global audience and respecting the claims of communities that regard such works as part of their national or cultural identity.
