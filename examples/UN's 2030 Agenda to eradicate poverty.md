## UN's 2030 Agenda to eradicate poverty

UN's 2030 Agenda to eradicate poverty through SDGs was criticised as it relies on a poverty line set so low by the World Bank that it allows governments to claim progress when there is none.

**Use:** IGOs often panders to the lowest denominator which makes milestone easily achieveable and less impactful for altruistic goals.
