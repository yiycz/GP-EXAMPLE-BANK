## The Beatles' Last Song, Finished by AI

In 2023, The Beatles released a 'final' song. AI pulled John Lennon's voice from a 1970s demo, and the track later won a Grammy.

**Use:** This supports the argument that AI is a tool whose value depends on how humans use it. The artistry stayed human.
