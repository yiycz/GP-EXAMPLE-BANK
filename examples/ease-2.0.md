## Enhancement for Active Seniors 2.0 (EASE 2.0)

Singapore expanded the **Enhancement for Active Seniors programme through EASE 2.0 in 2024**, subsidising home modifications that allow seniors to age more safely in place. Improvements include foldable shower seats, grab bars, lowered toilet entrance kerbs, ramps and other senior-friendly fittings. Eligible Singapore Citizen HDB households can receive subsidies covering **up to 95% of the cost**.

**Use:** This supports the argument that societies can respond to ageing populations by adapting the physical environment to seniors’ needs, allowing more elderly people to remain independent rather than relying primarily on institutional care.
