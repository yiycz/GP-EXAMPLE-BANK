## OxyContin and the Opioid Crisis

Purdue Pharma aggressively marketed OxyContin while downplaying its addictive risks, contributing to widespread overprescription and the opioid crisis in the United States. The case illustrates how profit-driven commercial pressures can distort the communication and application of scientific knowledge, especially when firms have strong financial incentives to maximise sales.

**Use:** This supports the argument that the commercialisation of science can compromise its ethical purpose when corporate greed encourages companies to suppress risks or manipulate scientific information at the expense of public welfare.
