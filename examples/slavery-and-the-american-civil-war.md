## Slavery and the American Civil War

The American Civil War, fought from **1861 to 1865**, emerged from deep divisions over slavery and its expansion in the United States. Although the war ultimately resulted in the abolition of slavery, it came at an immense human and material cost, causing more than one million casualties, including hundreds of thousands of deaths, while devastating communities and infrastructure across large parts of the country.

**Use:** This supports the argument that allowing severe social injustices and divisions to remain deeply entrenched can eventually produce destructive conflict, making early reform preferable to resolving such problems only after they reach a crisis point.
