## The Art of Charlie Chan Hock Chye Grant Withdrawal

In 2015, Singapore’s National Arts Council withdrew a grant for Sonny Liew’s graphic novel *The Art of Charlie Chan Hock Chye*, citing concerns over its “sensitive content” about Singapore’s political history. The decision sparked debate over the extent to which government bodies should influence artistic expression, especially when artworks critically examine the state or dominant historical narratives.

**Use:** This supports the argument that government regulation of the arts can protect social and political sensitivities, but it may also constrain artistic freedom when authorities are able to set the boundaries for works that critique them.
