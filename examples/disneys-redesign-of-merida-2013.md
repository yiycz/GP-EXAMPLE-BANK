## Disney's redesign of Merida (2013)

In 2013, Disney was criticised after redesigning Brave's Merida with a slimmer waist, larger eyes and a more glamorous appearance; more than 200,000 people petitioned against the makeover.

**Use:** This supports the argument that media representations can reinforce a narrow and unrealistic standard of female beauty, even when the original character was valued for resisting such stereotypes.
