## Former Philippine President Rodrigo Duterte

In March 2025, former Philippine president Rodrigo Duterte was arrested by Philippine authorities and transferred to ICC custody over alleged crimes against humanity committed during his anti-drug campaign, with the Court confirming the charges against him in April 2026.

However, ICC still has its limitations where International judicial institutions lack independent enforcement mechanisms, allowing national governments to prioritise diplomatic and strategic interests over their obligations under international law.

For instance: Despite Mongolia being a party to the Rome Statute, it welcomed Vladimir Putin for a state visit in September 2024 without executing the ICC's 2023 arrest warrant for alleged war crimes, prompting the Court to formally find Mongolia in non-compliance.


**Use:** International judicial institutions can hold even former national leaders subject to legal proceedings, demonstrating that political authority does not necessarily provide immunity from international criminal accountability.
