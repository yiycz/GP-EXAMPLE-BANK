## Transboundary Haze Pollution Agreement

The members have all ratified the ASEAN agreement on Transboundary Haze Pollution Agreement, but the treaty has failed to prevent the spread of the Haze. It was vague and lacked enforcement to hold the perpetrators of the haze accountable. 

**Use:** ASEAN's informal, non-confrontationist approach is often criticized for its inability to intervene into domestic affairs of its member countries.
