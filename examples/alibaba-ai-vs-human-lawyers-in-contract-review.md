## Alibaba AI vs Human Lawyers in Contract Review

In 2010, lawyers and law students from Zhejiang University’s Guanghua Law School were pitted against AI developed by Alibaba Group in reviewing contracts. The AI outperformed the human participants in speed and accuracy, returning results in under a minute with an accuracy rate of 96%. However, human lawyers were better at identifying nuanced problems that the AI failed to detect.

**Use:** This supports the argument that AI is likely to complement rather than completely replace skilled workers, as machines may outperform humans in speed and routine analysis while humans retain an advantage in handling ambiguity, nuance and complex judgement.
