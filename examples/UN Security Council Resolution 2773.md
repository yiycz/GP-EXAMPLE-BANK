## UN Security Council Resolution 2773

In February 2025, the UN Security Council **unanimously** adopted Resolution 2773, demanding that **M23 rebels** withdraw from occupied territories in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, yet by September 2026, the rebels **continued to control substantial territory** despite the resolution.

**Use:** Even legally binding resolutions may remain ineffective when the UN lacks the independent military capabilities to enforce compliance and must rely on member states to implement coercive measures.
