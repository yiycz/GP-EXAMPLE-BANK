## The Driverless Taxi Arrives

By 2025, Waymo's fully driverless taxis were providing more than 450,000 paid rides a week in US cities such as Phoenix and San Francisco. The vehicles use sensors and AI to read the road, and data suggests that they crash less often than human drivers. They still operate in only a limited number of cities after decades of promises.

**Use:** This supports the argument that AI can perform a human job more safely, while forcing passengers to surrender control to software and illustrating how technological delivery can lag far behind its promises.
