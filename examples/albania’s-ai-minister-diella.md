## Albania’s AI Minister Diella

In **2025, Albania appointed an AI-generated virtual minister named Diella** to oversee public procurement, with Prime Minister Edi Rama presenting the system as a way to reduce corruption and make government tenders more transparent. Diella had previously operated as a digital assistant on Albania’s e-government platform before being elevated to a cabinet role.

**Use:** This supports the argument that AI can improve governance by reducing opportunities for human corruption and increasing administrative efficiency, but delegating government decisions to algorithms also raises concerns about transparency, accountability and who should ultimately be responsible for public decisions.
