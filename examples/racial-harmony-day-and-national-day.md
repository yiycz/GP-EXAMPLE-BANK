## Racial Harmony Day And National Day
During major national events such as Racial Harmony Day and National Day, multiculturalism is often highlighted as being a key part of the Singaporean national identity. This is then accompanied by showcases of traditional crafts, such as music and dance performances.
Official collateral from the state (such as campaigns, brochures and guidelines) often highlight cross-awareness of other ethnic groups' traditional arts (or even learning and participation) as being a major step for achieving greater racial harmony among Singaporeans. This would include students participating in cultural societies outside of their own ethnicity.

**Use:** Traditional arts are important symbols of the cultures of Singapore’s major ethnic groups and are used in nation-building efforts to showcase both cultural strength and harmonious multicultural coexistence, indicating the Singaporean state's success in cultivating a diverse and peaceful society.
