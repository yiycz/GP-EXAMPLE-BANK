## WTO's Trade Policy Review Mechanism

The WTO's Trade Policy Review Mechanism subjects member states to periodic examinations of their trade policies, publishing independent assessments and allowing other members to raise questions.

For instance, during China's 2024 WTO Trade Policy Review, 72 WTO members participated in discussions and raised over 1,500 questions, scrutinising China's industrial subsidies, state-owned enterprises and opaque trade regulations.

However, its enforcement depends heavily on self-regulation and compliance of its member countries: WTO dispute panel found that US tariffs implemented on imported steel and aluminium were inconsistent with WTO's trading rules. The US government refused to remove the tariff. 

**Use:** IGOs provide a forum for discussions in which nations could share information to enhance transparency and facilitate issue linkages in order to facilitate cooperation.
