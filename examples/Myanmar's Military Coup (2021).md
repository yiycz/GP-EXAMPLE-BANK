## Myanmar's Military Coup (2021)

On **1 February 2021**, Myanmar's military, known as the **Tatmadaw**, led by General **Min Aung Hlaing**, staged a coup d'état to **forcibly oust the democratically elected civilian government** led by **Aung San Suu Kyi**, whose National League for Democracy (NLD) had secured a landslide victory in the **November 2020 general election**, with the military alleging electoral fraud to justify its seizure of power. The junta subsequently **detained Aung San Suu Kyi and President Win Myint, imposed a state of emergency and violently suppressed nationwide pro-democracy demonstrations**, using lethal force against protesters and plunging the country into prolonged political instability and armed conflict.

**Use:** Military intervention and the concentration of coercive power can undermine democratic institutions, allowing authoritarian leaders to suppress political opposition and override the electorate's will.
