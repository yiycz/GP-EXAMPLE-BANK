## SG Culture Pass

Under the **SG Culture Pass**, Singapore Citizens aged **18 and above received S$100 in credits from 1 September 2025** to spend on eligible local arts and heritage experiences. The credits are valid until **31 December 2028** and can be used for activities such as theatre, music, dance, visual arts and heritage programmes. From 2026, selected Singapore literature books at participating bookstores were also included.

**Use:** This supports the argument that governments can make the arts more accessible by reducing financial barriers to participation, while simultaneously supporting local artists and cultural organisations by stimulating demand.
