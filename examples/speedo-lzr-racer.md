## Speedo LZR Racer at the 2008 Beijing Olympics

At the 2008 Beijing Olympics, swimmers wearing Speedo's LZR Racer swimsuit won approximately 94% of swimming events, while 23 of the 25 world records broken during the Games were set by athletes wearing the suit. Designed to reduce drag and improve swimmers' efficiency in the water, the swimsuit demonstrated how technology could push the limits of athletic performance. However, sporting authorities later imposed restrictions on such high-technology swimsuits amid concerns that they provided an excessive competitive advantage.

**Use:** This supports the argument that while technology can enhance sporting performance, excessive technological advantages may undermine fair competition by making access to superior equipment as important as athletic ability.
