## Black Lives Matter protests


The United States is a liberal democracy where residents strongly believe in individuals' right to free speech and no strict laws are set in place as it is an unpopular measure among the people. As such, this resulted in the rise of false information regarding the Black Lives Matter protests uploaded on social media platforms. This eventually led to an exacerbation of the tensions between racial groups in America



**Use:** While it is true that laws can help to restrict platforms from spreading fake news, the growing trait of liberalisation in today's world has made it more difficult to restrict people's actions. Setting harsh laws that restrict one's freedom of expression may be seen in a bad light in liberal democracies, leading people to lose support for their governments.
