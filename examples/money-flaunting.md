## Money flaunting on social media

World-renowned celebrities and influencers like Cristiano Ronaldo showcase luxurious lifestyles with designer clothes and private jets, while smaller creators spotlight new tech and beauty products through unboxing videos. This trend subtly normalises excessive spending and an insidious culture of consumerism. Consequently, social media pressures individuals to alter their financial decisions, amplifying their spending to portray a false image of wealth.

**Use:** The culture of consumerism has also increased social media’s influence on people’s financial decisions today.
