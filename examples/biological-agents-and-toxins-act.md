## Biological Agents and Toxins Act (BATA)

Singapore’s **Biological Agents and Toxins Act (2005)** regulates the possession, use, import, transfer and transportation of biological agents and toxins that may threaten human health. It also imposes safety and security requirements on their handling, with different biological agents classified according to their level of risk.

**Use:** This supports the argument that potentially dangerous scientific research requires strong biosafety and biosecurity safeguards, even when such research may generate valuable scientific or medical discoveries.
