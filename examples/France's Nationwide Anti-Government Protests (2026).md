## France's Nationwide Anti-Government Protests (2026)

Example: In September 2026, nationwide protests erupted in France following teacher strikes over **underfunded public schools, teacher shortages and deteriorating educational facilities**, subsequently escalating into a broader movement against President Emmanuel Macron's government over **rising living costs, economic inequality and inadequate public services**. On 17 October 2026, approximately 550,000 demonstrators participated in nationwide protests according to French authorities, with some demonstrations descending into violent clashes, vandalism and mass arrests, prompting the government to promise education reforms while facing mounting political pressure ahead of the 2027 presidential election.

Use: Governments face competing pressures to maintain fiscal sustainability while meeting growing public demands for social welfare, as perceived failures to provide essential services and alleviate economic hardship can provoke widespread unrest and undermine political stability.
