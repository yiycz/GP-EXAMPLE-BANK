## Singapore's Condemnation of Russia's Invasion of Ukraine

Constructivism in IR is social theory which emphasizes the role of ideas, norms and identities in shaping state behaviour and the international system, rather than solely relying on material factors like power, self-interests. 

In simpler terms, what states believe is right, acceptable or legitimate can influence how they behave, even when certain actions do not maximise their immediate material interests. Singapore's condemnation also reflects its commitment to the principle that all sovereign states deserve respect for their territorial integrity, regardless of their military or economic power.

At the UN General Assembly in September 2023, Singapore's Foreign Minister Vivian Balakrishnan condemned Russia's invasion of Ukraine as a “flagrant violation of international law”, reaffirming Singapore's commitment to national sovereignty and territorial integrity.

Singapore's Foreign Minister Vivian Balakrishnan reiterated at the kent ridge ministerial formum that Singapore's foreign policy is not be neutral, but to act in a principled and consistent way based on long-term national interests. 

He states "We do not take sides, we hold principles"


**Use:** Smaller states have a vested interest in upholding a rules-based international order, as international law protects their sovereignty against aggression by more powerful nations.
