## Coldplay and Taylor Swift concerts in Singapore (2024)

Singapore's economy was boosted by increased tourism activity in 2024 when Coldplay and Taylor Swift held concerts in the country. Singapore recorded four million international visitor arrivals in the first three months of 2024, a 50% increase from the same period in 2023.

**Use:** This supports the argument that the arts possess substantial economic value because major cultural events attract overseas visitors and generate spending across tourism, hospitality and retail.
