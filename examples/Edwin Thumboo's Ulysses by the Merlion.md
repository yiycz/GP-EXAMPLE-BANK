## Edwin Thumboo's Ulysses by the Merlion

Edwin Thumboo's poem portrays the Greek mythological hero Ulysses encountering Singapore's Merlion, which symbolises the country's transformation into a prosperous, multicultural nation. 

(Ulysses is a Greek mythological hero who travels extensively before returning to his homeland, Ithaca. Similarly, Singapore is portrayed as a society formed by people who migrated from different places and gradually developed a shared sense of belonging.)

**Use:** Music can find a cohesive narrative that unites people and mobilise collective actions in the face of crises; Music evokes emotion attachments to the nation.
