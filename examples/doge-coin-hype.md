## Doge Coin hype


During the 2021 crypto boom, Elon Musk's social media hype drove Dogecoin's global popularity. Driven by FOMO and quick profit seeking, buyers ignored its lack of intrinsic value. Consequently, the coin surged 8,000% to a 73-cent peak, before plummeting to 25 cents two months later.

**Use:** People’s financial decisions continue to be influenced through popular trends on social media. Efforts of regulations to ensure that people are completely unaffected become considerably futile in the face of the sheer amount of constantly emerging content that promotes ideas that the individual willingly subscribes to.
