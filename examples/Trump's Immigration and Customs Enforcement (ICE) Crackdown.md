## Trump's Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) Crackdown

Since returning to office in **January 2025**, US President **Donald Trump** has intensified immigration enforcement through **Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE)**, expanding immigration raids, detention operations and deportations, with arrests reaching a record **nearly 51,000 in August 2026**, although deportations remained at approximately **1,200 per day**. According to Reuters, **fewer than one-quarter of those arrested in July 2026 had criminal convictions**, while the administration's aggressive enforcement tactics and deportation policies have attracted criticism over **due process, civil liberties and the treatment of migrants**, even as the administration maintains that stricter enforcement is essential for border security and immigration control.

**Use:** Government efforts to safeguard national security and enforce immigration laws can create tensions with civil liberties and human rights, highlighting the importance of proportionality and institutional accountability.
