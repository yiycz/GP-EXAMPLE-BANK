## Belize and Maldives public condemnation of Israel

Smaller states such as Belize and the Maldives publicly condemned Israel's alleged human rights violations in Gaza at the UN General Assembly in 2024 and 2025 respectively, with Belize suspending diplomatic ties and the Maldives banning Israeli passport holders.

The fact they stood to gain little in light economic, political or security benefits and place them under the hostile gaze of the us and Israel is indeed a laudable attempt to defend universal human rights. 

**Use:** International cooperation can sometimes be genuine and sincere in the area of defending universal human rights.
