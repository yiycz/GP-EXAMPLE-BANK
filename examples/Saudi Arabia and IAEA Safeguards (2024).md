## Saudi Arabia and IAEA Safeguards (2024)

Saudi Arabia had operated under a Small Quantities Protocol (SQP), a lighter safeguards arrangement for states with little nuclear material, which reduced some reporting and inspection requirements. The IAEA had long regarded the old SQP system as too weak and encouraged states to strengthen or rescind it. In July 2024, Saudi Arabia asked to rescind its SQP, and from 31 December 2024 it moved to full implementation of its Comprehensive Safeguards Agreement, accepting greater IAEA oversight.

Use: Although the IAEA could not force Saudi Arabia to change, its standards, expertise and institutional pressure helped encourage the state to accept stronger international scrutiny. IOs can therefore shape state behaviour and improve compliance even without coercive power
