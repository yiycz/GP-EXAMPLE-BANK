## He Jiankui's gene-edited babies

Chinese scientist He Jiankui announced the birth of gene-edited babies after altering embryos using CRISPR, provoking widespread scientific condemnation. He even forged ethical documents and misled the doctors involved. 

**Use:** The case demonstrates why scientific capability must be constrained by ethical oversight, transparency and respect for consent. It also suggests that scientists may only chase glory and fame, even at the expense of ethics.
