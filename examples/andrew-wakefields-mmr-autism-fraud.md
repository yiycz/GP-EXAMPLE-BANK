## Andrew Wakefield's MMR–Autism Fraud

British doctor Andrew Wakefield published a paper claiming a link between the MMR vaccine and autism. The paper was later retracted after serious ethical violations and data manipulation were uncovered, yet the claim continued to fuel vaccine hesitancy.

**Use:** This supports the argument that academic dishonesty can obstruct scientific progress because a fraudulent claim may continue to influence public behaviour and contaminate later research even after it is withdrawn.
