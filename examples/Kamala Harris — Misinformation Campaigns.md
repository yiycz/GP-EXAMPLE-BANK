## Kamala Harris — Misinformation Campaigns

Misinformation campaigns targeting former Vice President and presidential candidate Kamala Harris illustrate how technological advances make it increasingly difficult for citizens to verify whether leaders are acting honestly. False claims that Harris was not a ‘natural-born citizen’ and misleading narratives questioning her racial identity spread widely on social media, despite clear constitutional and factual evidence proving her eligibility and heritage. These viral falsehoods demonstrate how online platforms amplify misinformation, causing many Americans to doubt the legitimacy of leaders who are in fact genuine about serving the community. Such examples show that technological advances have enabled misinformation to spread faster and reach larger audiences than ever before, making it increasingly difficult for citizens to discern truth from falsehood and to trust those in power today.

**Use:** The spread of misinformation due to technological advances and a persistent lack of transparency from those in power make it increasingly difficult for the public to verify whether leaders are acting honestly. Hence, this uncertainty itself becomes a significant driver of distrust, even when outright wrongdoing cannot always be conclusively proven.
