## The #MeToo Movement

In 2017, investigative reporting—including Ronan Farrow's work on allegations against Harvey Weinstein—helped expose the scale of sexual harassment in the American workplace. Alyssa Milano then popularised the #MeToo hashtag, encouraging survivors to share their experiences and turning isolated testimonies into a global movement.

**Use:** This supports the argument that investigative journalism and social media can reinforce each other: reporting exposes concealed abuse, while digital participation transforms individual testimony into sustained public pressure.
