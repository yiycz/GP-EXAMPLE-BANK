## Stuxnet, the First Cyberweapon

Around 2010, the Stuxnet computer worm, widely believed to have been built by the United States and Israel, targeted Iran's Natanz nuclear site. It secretly accelerated enrichment centrifuges while telling operators that everything was normal, destroying about 1,000 machines. It was the first known cyberweapon to cause physical destruction.

**Use:** This supports the argument that reliance on connected technology creates a hidden strategic weakness, because code can silently attack the systems running essential infrastructure even without soldiers crossing a border.
