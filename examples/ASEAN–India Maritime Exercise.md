## ASEAN–India Maritime Exercise

In May 2023, Singapore co-hosted the inaugural ASEAN–India Maritime Exercise as ASEAN’s coordinator for dialogue relations with India, bringing the Indian Navy together with navies from ASEAN member states.

**Use:** Singapore used its coordinator role to connect India with ASEAN militaries and build confidence on shared maritime security challenges.
