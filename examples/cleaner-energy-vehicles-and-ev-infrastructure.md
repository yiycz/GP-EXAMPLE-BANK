## Cleaner-Energy Vehicles and EV Infrastructure under the Singapore Green Plan 2030

Under the **Singapore Green Plan 2030**, Singapore aims for **all new car and taxi registrations to be cleaner-energy models from 2030**, while targeting **60,000 electric-vehicle charging points nationwide by 2030**. All HDB towns were also targeted to become EV-ready by 2025, helping to make electric-vehicle ownership more practical for residents.

**Use:** This supports the argument that governments can accelerate the transition to cleaner transport by combining regulation with infrastructure investment, making environmentally friendly alternatives both increasingly necessary and practically accessible.
