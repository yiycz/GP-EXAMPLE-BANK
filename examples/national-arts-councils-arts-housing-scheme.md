## National Arts Council's Arts Housing Scheme

The National Arts Council's Arts Housing Scheme subsidises studio space and provides affordable workspaces to local artists and arts organisations, enabling them to focus on artistic creation instead of bearing prohibitively high rental costs.

**Use:** This supports the argument that public support for the arts is necessary because high operating costs may otherwise prevent socially valuable but less commercially profitable artistic work from being produced.
