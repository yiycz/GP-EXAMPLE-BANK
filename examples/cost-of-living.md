## Cost of Living and Financial Pressure in Singapore

Cost of living remains a major social concern in Singapore. An **Institute of Policy Studies survey following GE2025** found that cost of living was the most important election issue, with **74% of respondents** regarding it as very important. Although CPI-All Items inflation declined from **4.8% in 2023 to 0.9% in 2025**, households may continue to experience financial pressure because slower inflation does not reverse the price increases accumulated in previous years.

**Use:** This supports the argument that falling inflation does not necessarily eliminate cost-of-living pressures, and governments may still need to intervene to protect household purchasing power and vulnerable groups.
