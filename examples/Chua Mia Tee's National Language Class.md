## Chua Mia Tee's National Language Class

Chua Mia Tee's National Language Class depicts young Singaporeans learning Malay during the country's transition to self-government. National museum of Singapore make SG' nation-building tangible to subsequent generations, allowing citizens to understand historical struggles and aspirations that shape their identity

**Use:** Museums serve as an important repository of curated art works that society deems valuable and presentative of a certain era.
