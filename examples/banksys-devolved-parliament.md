## Banksy's Devolved Parliament

Banksy is the pseudonym of a British street artist whose graffiti is frequently read as social and political commentary. His work satirises greed, hypocrisy and alienation. Devolved Parliament suggests that political debate is uncivilised, chaotic and driven by base instincts rather than logic.

**Use:** This supports the argument that visual art can criticise political institutions through satire, making complex concerns about leadership and public debate immediately accessible.
