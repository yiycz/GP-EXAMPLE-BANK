## Pink Dot SG — LGBTQ+ Rights in Singapore

Since its establishment in **2009**, **Pink Dot SG** has organised annual gatherings at **Hong Lim Park** to advocate greater acceptance and equality for Singapore's LGBTQ+ community, with its mobilisation contributing to wider public discussions surrounding the **2022 repeal of Section 377A**, which criminalised sexual acts between men. At its **18th rally on 27 June 2026**, thousands gathered under the theme **“Come Get Personal”**, with more than **20 LGBTQ+ community organisations** sharing personal experiences through interactive booths and storytelling activities, demonstrating the movement's continued efforts to challenge social prejudice and promote inclusivity.

**Use:** Civil society movements can amplify the voices of marginalised communities and encourage political and social reforms by mobilising public support and challenging prevailing social norms.
