## The Smart City That Collapsed

In 2017, Google's sister company Sidewalk Labs planned a high-tech district on Toronto's waterfront, using sensors to track traffic, waste and movement. Residents questioned who would own the data and who would watch them, while privacy experts left the project. Trust collapsed, and Sidewalk Labs cancelled the plan in 2020.

**Use:** This supports the argument that smart cities create more problems than benefits when they treat residents as data rather than citizens; governance, ownership and trust matter more than the sophistication of the gadgets.
