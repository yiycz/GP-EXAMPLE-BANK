## Google Ruled a Monopoly

In August 2024, a US court ruled Google an illegal monopoly after finding that it controlled nearly 90% of web searches and paid billions to remain the default search engine on phones and browsers. The arrangements locked out rivals and allowed Google to raise advertising prices. It was the largest technology-monopoly ruling in decades.

**Use:** This supports the argument that concentrated technology power requires public checks, because one dominant company can quietly shape what billions of people see and choose while suppressing competition.
