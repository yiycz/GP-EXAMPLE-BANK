## ICRC-facilitated Detainee Releases in Yemen (2023)

Between **14 and 16 April 2023**, the **International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC)** facilitated the release and repatriation of **869 conflict-related detainees** through **15 flights connecting six cities in Yemen and Saudi Arabia**, following negotiations involving parties to Yemen's prolonged civil war. An additional **104 detainees were released on 17 April**, bringing the total to **973**, demonstrating how cooperation between belligerents and neutral humanitarian organisations can uphold the **humanitarian principles of jus in bello by protecting people no longer actively participating in hostilities**, even while broader armed conflict continues.

**Use:** International humanitarian law can mitigate the suffering caused by armed conflict by protecting detainees and encouraging humanitarian cooperation between opposing parties, regardless of their political disagreements.
