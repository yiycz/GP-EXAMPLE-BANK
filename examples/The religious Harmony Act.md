## The religious Harmony Act

The religious Harmony Act prohibits individuals or other groups from making offensive remarks or allows the state to censor content that is deemed harmful to racial harmony. 

In October 2022, Singapore's IMDA refused classification to Ken Kwek's film #LookAtMe, which portrays a protagonist confronting a Christian pastor over his stance on homosexuality, arguing that the film could denigrate a religious community and provoke religious tensions.

**Use:** Governments need to prevent chaos and anarchy, large interest groups with competing goals and ideologies requires government intervention to prevent them from subsuming small groups.
