## Viya's tax-evasion penalty

Chinese e-commerce influencer Huang Wei, popularly known as Viya, was ordered to pay 1.34 billion yuan in taxes and fines for tax evasion despite previously being praised for charity and poverty-relief contributions.

**Use:** This supports the argument that media celebrities are held to high moral and legal standards because their influence makes their misconduct especially visible and socially consequential.
