## Alfian Sa'at's Asian Boys Vol.

Asian Boys Vol. by internationally recognised Singaporean playwright Alfian Sa'at examines LGBTQ+ issues that may be regarded as socially and politically sensitive.

**Use:** This supports the argument that the arts can give visibility to marginalised communities, challenge prevailing social norms and encourage discussion of issues that society may prefer to avoid.
