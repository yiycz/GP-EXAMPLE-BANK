## Fusion's Breakthrough Moment

In December 2022, a US laboratory produced more energy from a nuclear-fusion reaction than the lasers delivered to the fuel: about 3 megajoules were released after roughly 2 megajoules were applied. It was the first laboratory net-energy gain from fusion. However, the lasers themselves used far more power than the reaction returned, and a working power plant remains decades away.

**Use:** This supports the argument that science advances through slow but genuine steps, while headlines can turn a real milestone into an exaggerated promise of limitless clean energy.
