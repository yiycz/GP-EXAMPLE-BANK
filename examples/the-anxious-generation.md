## The Anxious Generation

In late 2024, Australia passed the world's first law barring under-16s from platforms such as TikTok and Instagram; the restrictions took effect in December 2025. Companies can face fines of up to A$50 million if they fail to keep young teenagers out, although users may try to bypass the rules through VPNs. Lawmakers linked social media and addictive app design to rising teenage anxiety and depression.

**Use:** This supports the argument that technology can erode autonomy and wellbeing: tools intended to connect teenagers may deepen loneliness and addiction, so freedom can mean freedom from a device rather than unrestricted access. However, a ban may remove support networks and remain difficult to enforce.
