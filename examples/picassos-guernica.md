## Picasso's Guernica

Guernica (1937) by Pablo Picasso. His work is regarded by many art critics as the most moving and powerful anti-war painting in history for including his experience of  the war through various abstract techniques. 

**Use:** This supports the argument that art provides a powerful platform for political expression by condemning warfare and communicating its human cost more emotionally than factual reporting alone. This also suggests that technology will not be able to understand the pain of war which surfaces in the technique and expressions that Picasso uses in his work, thereby proving that the creation of art is inherently designed for humans and years of skill cannot simply be condensed into lines of code.
