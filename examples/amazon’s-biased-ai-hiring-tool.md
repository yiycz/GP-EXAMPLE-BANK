## Amazon’s Biased AI Hiring Tool

In 2018, Amazon scrapped an AI-driven recruitment tool after discovering that it discriminated against female applicants. The system had been trained on resumes submitted over a 10-year period, many of which came from men because of the male-dominated nature of the technology industry. As a result, the AI learned patterns that favoured male candidates and penalised indicators associated with women.

**Use:** This supports the argument that AI can reproduce and even amplify existing social biases when it is trained on historically biased data, showing that technological systems are not inherently neutral and still require human oversight.
