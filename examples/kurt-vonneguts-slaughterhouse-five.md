## Kurt Vonnegut's Slaughterhouse-Five

Slaughterhouse-Five recalls real events such as the firebombing of Dresden during the Second World War. It has faced numerous censorship attempts because of its provocative and frank representation of war.

**Use:** This supports the argument that literature can challenge sanitised narratives of war and uphold freedom of expression by confronting readers with disturbing historical realities.
