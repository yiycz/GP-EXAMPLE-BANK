## Singapore's Chingay Parade

Chingay is Singapore's iconic annual street parade celebrating multiculturalism, art and community through performances such as Peranakan showcases, Malay kompang processions and dragon dances.

**Use:** This supports the argument that the arts help people appreciate and understand different cultures, creating common ground that strengthens the social fabric and national cohesion.
