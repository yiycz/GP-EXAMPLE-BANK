## MAS

In Singapore, The Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) acts as the regulatory body that ensures local social media users are warned against any potential financial misinformation or any unsubstantiated claims of supposed profits from promoted financial instruments by online financial content creators. Similar to the MAS’s role in Singapore, the Securities and Exchange Commission in the US imposes stringent rules that ascertains financial information shared online is factually accurate, transparent, and in compliance with existing financial laws.



**Use:** There are strict regulations put in place that manage the promotion of financial instruments on social media.
