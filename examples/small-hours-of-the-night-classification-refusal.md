## Small Hours of the Night classification refusal

Local filmmaker Daniel Hui's Small Hours of the Night, a film based on a real-life censorship case from the 1980s, was refused classification for public screening and distribution in Singapore.

**Use:** This supports the argument that censorship may preserve the status quo by preventing artists from revisiting politically sensitive events and limiting the range of perspectives available to society.
