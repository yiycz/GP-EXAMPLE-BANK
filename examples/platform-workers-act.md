## Platform Workers Act

From **1 January 2025**, Singapore’s **Platform Workers Act** introduced statutory protections for ride-hailing and delivery platform workers. These include work-injury compensation, CPF protections and rights to representation, while platform operators assume corresponding responsibilities such as providing work-injury insurance and making CPF contributions.

**Use:** This supports the argument that labour regulation must adapt to technological changes in the economy, especially when gig workers depend heavily on digital platforms but may lack protections traditionally associated with conventional employment.
