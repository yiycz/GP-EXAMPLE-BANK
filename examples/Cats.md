## Cats

"Cats" is a whimsical and riveting musicality that lacks a concrete story line. Rather, it focuses on its musical and theatrical aspects. 

**Use:** The arts can provide a sense of escapism. They are not avenues that one would be caught up with the dull and monotony of life routines. Rather, they provide a safe space in which one can attain catharsis from and escape from the harsh reality.
