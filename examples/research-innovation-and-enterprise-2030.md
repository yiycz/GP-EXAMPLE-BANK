## Research, Innovation and Enterprise 2030 (RIE2030)

Singapore committed **S$37 billion** to research, innovation and enterprise over five years beginning in April 2026, equivalent to approximately **1% of GDP**. The programme aims to strengthen basic research, develop scientific talent and translate research into economic and national benefits, reflecting Singapore’s view of science as both intellectual inquiry and a strategic tool for economic competitiveness, technological independence and solving national problems.

**Use:** This supports the argument that governments invest in science not merely to expand knowledge, but also to strengthen economic competitiveness, technological capabilities and national resilience.
