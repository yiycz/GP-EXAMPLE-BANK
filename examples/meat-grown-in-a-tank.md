## Meat Grown in a Tank

In 2023, the United States approved the first sale of cultivated meat: real chicken grown from animal cells in steel tanks without raising or killing an animal. The first servings appeared in high-end restaurants. Production remains tiny and very expensive despite promises that it could reduce emissions, land use and animal suffering.

**Use:** This supports the argument that science can imagine a more sustainable and humane food system, but inventing a solution is easier than scaling it cheaply enough to fulfil its promise.
