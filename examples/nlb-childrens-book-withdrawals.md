## NLB children's-book withdrawals

Singapore's National Library Board withdrew And Tango Makes Three, The White Swan Express: A Story About Adoption and Who's In My Family after a public complaint. Their themes of homosexuality and unconventional families were considered inappropriate for children.

**Use:** This supports the argument that censorship may be used to protect prevailing ideas of public morality and conventional family values, although it may also restrict representation of diverse families.
