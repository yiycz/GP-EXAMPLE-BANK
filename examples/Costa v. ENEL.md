## Costa v. ENEL

In 1964, the European Court of Justice ruled in Costa v. ENEL that European Community law takes precedence over conflicting Italian legislation, even when the national law was enacted later. 

It established the principle of supremacy (primacy) of EU law, meaning that EU law takes precedence over conflicting national laws of its member states

**Use:** This highlights the supranational authority of the EU, as member states have voluntarily limited aspects of their national sovereignty by accepting a common legal framework that overrides conflicting domestic legislation.
