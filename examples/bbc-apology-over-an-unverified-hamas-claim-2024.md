## BBC apology over an unverified Hamas claim (2024)

Early in 2024, amid the Israel-Hamas war, the BBC apologised for reporting an unverified Hamas claim that the Israeli army had carried out 'summary executions' of Palestinian civilians in Gaza.

**Use:** This supports the argument that even established media organisations can mislead the public when they publish serious claims before sufficient verification.
