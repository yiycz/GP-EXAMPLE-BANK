## Antigone

Sophocles' Antigone depicted a tragedy related to family curses and civil wars, exploring the dilemma between obedience to laws and moral duty. Antigone defies the king's decree to bury his brother. 

**Use:** Literature can contribute moralistic and ethical development of people in society by providing a safe space for people to examine societal issues and moral dilemmas.
