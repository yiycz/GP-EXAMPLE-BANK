## A good child

**"A good child"** is a **brave and poignant** look at the life of a Singaporean **drag king**, who **struggle with** his conservation family over his career choices, although there is **greater acceptance** of the IGBTQ+ community.

**Use:** The arts serve as a powerful form of **social commentary** that highlights social issues and raises public awareness of them.
