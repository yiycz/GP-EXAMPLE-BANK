## Corruption in Nigeria

Nigeria suffered from extensive political corruption following its independence in 1960. It has been estimated that close to **US$400 billion** was stolen from the country's public accounts between 1960 and 1999. Despite Nigeria's vast natural-resource wealth, such corruption diverted resources away from infrastructure, healthcare, education and productive investment, undermining the country's development.

**Use:** This supports the argument that entrenched corruption can severely impede economic and social development, as public resources intended to improve citizens' welfare are diverted for private gain.
