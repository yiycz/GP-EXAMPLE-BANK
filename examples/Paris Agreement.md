## Paris Agreement

At COP21, 195 parties adopted the Paris Agreement, committing to keep global warming well below 2°C while pursuing a 1.5°C limit, with countries required to submit progressively more ambitious climate plans every five years. -> how international negotiations can reconcile competing national interests and establish common benchmarks for collective action.

On 20 January 2025, US President Donald Trump ordered the United States' second withdrawal from the Paris Agreement, citing national economic interests, with the withdrawal officially taking effect on 27 January 2026. (how international environmental cooperation can be undermined by conflicting national interests and the lack of enforceable commitments, as even major powers can unilaterally withdraw from multilateral agreements.)
