## The Humanoid Robot Race

In 2024, Boston Dynamics unveiled a nimble all-electric Atlas robot, while Tesla demonstrated its Optimus robot folding shirts and sorting objects. Companies promise humanoid robots for work that is unsafe, repetitive or boring. The demonstrations are impressive, but reliable and affordable humanoid robots remain years away.

**Use:** This supports the argument that automation is extending from intellectual tasks into physical work, although impressive demonstrations may overstate how quickly useful humanoid robots will become dependable and affordable.
