## Hawk-Eye in Tennis and Cricket

Hawk-Eye uses camera-based tracking technology to determine the trajectory and landing position of a ball with high accuracy. In sports such as tennis, players can challenge disputed line calls and have the system determine whether the ball landed in or out, reducing reliance on human judgement alone.

**Use:** This supports the argument that technology can enhance fairness in sport by reducing human error and providing more objective decisions.
