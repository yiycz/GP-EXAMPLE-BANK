## US Occupation and De-Baathification of Iraq (2003)

Following the **US-led invasion of Iraq in March 2003** and the overthrow of Saddam Hussein, the American-led **Coalition Provisional Authority**, headed by **Paul Bremer**, implemented sweeping **de-Baathification policies and Coalition Provisional Authority Order No. 2**, which dissolved Iraq's military institutions and excluded many former Baath Party members from government employment. These policies contributed to the **marginalisation of former military personnel, a severe security vacuum and the rise of armed insurgencies**, according to subsequent assessments, illustrating a failure to adequately fulfil the **jus post bellum ideal of establishing a stable, inclusive and sustainable post-conflict political order**.

**Use:** Military victory does not necessarily produce lasting peace, as poorly designed post-war reconstruction policies can exacerbate political grievances, undermine institutional stability and create conditions for renewed conflict.
