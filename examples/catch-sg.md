## Catch.sg

As part of **Our SG Arts Plan (2023–2027)**, the National Arts Council and major cultural institutions launched **Catch.sg in 2023**, a one-stop digital platform where users can discover arts and cultural events, receive personalised recommendations and purchase tickets. Smaller arts groups and individual artists can promote their programmes on the platform free of charge, helping less-established practitioners gain visibility and reach wider audiences.

**Use:** This supports the argument that governments can increase participation in the arts by improving awareness and discoverability, while giving smaller artists greater access to audiences without directly determining what people should consume.
