## Replika and Emotional Dependence on AI Companions

**Replika** is an AI chatbot designed to act as a friend or romantic companion. Some users have developed strong emotional attachments to the chatbot, reporting sadness, withdrawal-like feelings and psychological dependence when unable to interact with it. A 2025 study analysing thousands of online discussions described a potential cycle of **“digital entrapment”**, in which increasingly personalised interactions can reinforce dependence and distort expectations of human relationships.

**Use:** This supports the argument that technology designed to alleviate loneliness can paradoxically deepen social withdrawal when frictionless artificial companionship begins to replace the difficulties, compromises and emotional demands of genuine human relationships.
