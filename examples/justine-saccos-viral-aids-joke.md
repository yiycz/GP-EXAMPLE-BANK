## Justine Sacco's viral AIDS joke

Before flying to South Africa, PR executive Justine Sacco posted an offensive joke about AIDS to around 170 Twitter followers. While she was in the air, it went viral under #HasJustineLandedYet, and she was subsequently fired.

**Use:** This supports the argument that social media can impose enormous reputational and professional punishment before context and proportionality are carefully considered.
