## Nuclear Power's Comeback

In 2024, Microsoft agreed to restart the Three Mile Island nuclear plant to power its AI data centres. Data centres already use about 4% of US electricity, and their demand is rising quickly. Nuclear power is low-carbon but continues to carry concerns about accidents and waste.

**Use:** This supports the argument that science can provide clean power at scale, while complicating the idea that progress serves the public good when a risky energy source is revived mainly to feed private AI.
