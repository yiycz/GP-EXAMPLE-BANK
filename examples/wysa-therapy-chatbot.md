## Singapore’s Therapy Chatbot for MOE Teachers

Singapore introduced a therapy chatbot for stressed and overworked MOE teachers, but it drew criticism for being unhelpful and, in some cases, aggravating to users seeking mental-health support. While the AI chatbot Wysa could guide users through self-care and self-management exercises, it lacked the emotional range and empathy needed to respond appropriately to users’ distress or personal rants, sometimes leaving struggling teachers feeling even more frustrated.

**Use:** This supports the argument that AI can assist with basic mental-health support, but its inability to exercise genuine empathy and emotional judgement limits its effectiveness in situations that require sensitive human understanding.
