## Enhanced CPF Housing Grant 

The government increased housing grants to a maximum of S$120,000 for eligible first-time families purchasing HDB flats, reducing the financial burden of home ownership. 

The grant improves housing affordability, with over 80% of first-time HDB buyers with HDB loans spending less than a quarter of their monthly income on mortgage repayments in 2023, although rising resale prices remain a concern
