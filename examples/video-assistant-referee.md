## Video Assistant Referee (VAR) in Football

Video Assistant Referee (VAR) technology allows football officials to review video footage when making important decisions, such as whether a goal should stand, whether a foul warrants a penalty or red card, or whether a player was offside. By giving referees access to additional visual evidence, VAR can reduce human error and improve the accuracy of controversial decisions.

**Use:** This supports the argument that technology can improve fairness in sport by helping officials make more accurate and evidence-based decisions.
