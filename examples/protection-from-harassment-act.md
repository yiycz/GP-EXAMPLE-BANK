## Protection from Harassment Act (POHA)

Singapore introduced the **Protection from Harassment Act (POHA) in 2014** to provide criminal and civil protection against harassment, including threatening or abusive communications and unlawful stalking both offline and online. Amendments in 2019 also criminalised **doxxing**, while the specialised **Protection from Harassment Court** was established in 2021 to allow victims to seek remedies such as Protection Orders and Expedited Protection Orders more efficiently.

**Use:** This supports the argument that freedom of expression online is not absolute when communication directly threatens another person’s safety, privacy or welfare, and that legal intervention can be justified to protect victims from serious online harm.
