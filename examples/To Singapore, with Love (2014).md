## To Singapore, with Love (2014)

In September 2014, Singapore's Media Development Authority gave Tan Pin Pin's documentary _To Singapore, with Love_, featuring interviews with Singaporean political exiles, a Not Allowed for All Ratings classification on national-security grounds, thereby barring public screenings.

**Use:** The restriction illustrates how state censorship can limit the public's access to alternative interpretations of national history and hinder meaningful discussion of politically sensitive events.
