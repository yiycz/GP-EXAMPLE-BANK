## Harper Lee's To kill a Mocking bird

Harper Lee's To kill a Mocking bird depicts social injustice done to a black man who was accused of raping a white woman during the Jim Crew era where racial segregation was institutionalized. -> racial profiling of a black man presume him to be guilty of a rape even he is innocent. 

**Use:** The arts serve as a powerful form of social commentary that highlights social issues and raises public awareness of them.
