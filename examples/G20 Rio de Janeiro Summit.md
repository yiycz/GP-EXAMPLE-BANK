## G20 Rio de Janeiro Summit 

At the 2024 G20 Summit in Brazil, world leaders launched the Global Alliance Against Hunger and Poverty, bringing together 82 countries and numerous international organisations to coordinate financing, share policy expertise and accelerate poverty eradication.

At the 2024 G20 Summit in Brazil, world leaders reaffirmed their commitment to tripling global renewable energy capacity by 2030 and mobilising climate financing for developing countries, demonstrating collective efforts to combat climate change through coordinated policies and financial assistance.

At the 2024 G20 Summit in Brazil, world leaders reaffirmed their commitment to the Common Framework for Debt Treatments, facilitating coordinated debt restructuring for heavily indebted developing countries such as Zambia and Ghana to restore fiscal sustainability and support long-term development.

At the 2024 G20 Summit in Brazil, world leaders called for reforms to the UN Security Council to increase representation for developing regions, particularly Africa, while welcoming an additional Sub-Saharan African seat on the IMF Executive Board to promote more inclusive global decision-making.


**Use:** International forums facilitate collective action by pooling financial resources and expertise to help poorer countries develop sustainable poverty-reduction programmes.
