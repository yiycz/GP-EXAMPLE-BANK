## China’s Restrictions on AI Virtual Companions

China’s **Interim Measures for the Administration of Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services**, which took effect on **15 July 2026**, introduced safeguards against excessive emotional dependence on AI companions. Providers cannot offer minors virtual relatives, partners or other virtual intimate relationships and must provide youth modes and usage-time controls. Services must also warn users showing signs of excessive dependence, while continuous interaction exceeding two hours triggers a usage reminder.

**Use:** This supports the argument that governments may justifiably regulate emotionally persuasive technologies when their addictive design risks undermining youth welfare, mental health and genuine human relationships.
