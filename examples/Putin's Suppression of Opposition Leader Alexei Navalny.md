## Putin's Suppression of Opposition Leader Alexei Navalny

Russian opposition leader **Alexei Navalny**, a prominent critic of President **Vladimir Putin** who exposed alleged government corruption, survived a **2020 poisoning**, but was **arrested upon returning to Russia in January 2021** and subsequently received a **19-year prison sentence on extremism charges in August 2023**, which human rights organisations condemned as politically motivated. Navalny **died on 16 February 2024 in a remote Arctic penal colony**, prompting international condemnation and allegations of Kremlin responsibility, which Russian authorities denied, while highlighting concerns that **concentrated executive power, politically motivated prosecutions and restrictions on opposition activity** can undermine political pluralism and accountability.

**Use:** Authoritarian governments can exploit their control over state institutions, including the judiciary and security apparatus, to suppress political opposition, consolidate power and undermine democratic accountability.
