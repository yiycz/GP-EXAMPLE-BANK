## Gulf War — UN-authorised Intervention in Kuwait (1991)

Following Iraq's **invasion and occupation of Kuwait on 2 August 1990**, the **UN Security Council adopted Resolution 678 on 29 November 1990**, authorising member states cooperating with Kuwait to use **“all necessary means”** to restore international peace and security if Iraq failed to withdraw by 15 January 1991. Consequently, a **US-led multinational coalition launched Operation Desert Storm in January 1991**, eventually expelling Iraqi forces from Kuwait in February, demonstrating how military intervention may satisfy **jus ad bellum when explicitly authorised by the UN Security Council**.

**Use:** International law can legitimise the use of military force when undertaken through collective security mechanisms to reverse unlawful aggression and defend the sovereignty of states.
