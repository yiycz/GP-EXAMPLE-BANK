## Love and Deepspace and Virtual Romance in Singapore

In **2025, CNA reported** that some young Singaporean women had developed highly immersive relationships with male characters in the Chinese mobile game *Love and Deepspace*. Players exchanged virtual rings, referred to characters as their “husbands”, organised birthday celebrations and sometimes spent thousands of dollars on in-game purchases. Singapore fans even funded digital billboards at **313@Somerset**. The characters are not AI chatbots but scripted digital avatars whose dialogue responds to player choices, with features such as supportive messages linked to users’ menstrual cycles.

**Use:** This supports the argument that highly immersive digital relationships can provide emotional comfort and community, but they may also encourage excessive emotional and financial investment in artificial relationships that cannot fully replicate genuine human connection.
