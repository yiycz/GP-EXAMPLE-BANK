## Dormitory Transition Scheme (2023)

The government introduced stricter dormitory standards, requiring existing migrant worker dormitories to provide more living space, improved sanitation and isolation facilities by 2030, with financial grants introduced in 2026 to support around 900 dormitories.

The scheme could significantly improve living conditions for migrant workers, potentially benefiting around 200,000 dormitory beds, although its gradual implementation means that many workers will not experience these improvements immediately.
