## Elderly Loneliness and Social Isolation in Singapore

The number of **one-person households among people aged 65 and above more than doubled from 42,100 in 2014 to 87,200 in 2024**. A local study also found that older adults living alone were about twice as likely to report depressive symptoms as those who did not live alone. In 2025, around **40% of seniors aged 75 and above lived either alone or only with other seniors**, and around one-quarter of this group required help with at least one activity of daily living.

**Use:** This supports the argument that an ageing society must address not only seniors' physical and financial needs but also social isolation and mental well-being, strengthening the case for community-based support and active-ageing programmes.
