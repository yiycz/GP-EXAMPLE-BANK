## AI Wins an Art Prize

In 2022, Jason Allen entered an image made with Midjourney in a Colorado art competition and won first place in its category. He used hundreds of prompts and edited the result, while artists argued that a machine rather than a person had made the work. The judges said that they would have selected it even if they had known AI was involved.

**Use:** This supports the argument that AI can compete in work once considered uniquely creative, but it may be better understood as a powerful brush than an artist because human taste, intention and judgement still shape the result.
