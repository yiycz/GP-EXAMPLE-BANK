## US Veto of Gaza Ceasefire Resolution

On 18 September 2025, the US vetoed a UN Security Council resolution demanding an immediate Gaza ceasefire and unrestricted humanitarian assistance, despite support from the other 14 Council members

**Use:**  The veto power of permanent Security Council members can prevent the UN from adopting legally binding measures against states, undermining its ability to enforce international law when powerful nations' interests are involved.
