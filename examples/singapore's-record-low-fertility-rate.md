## Singapore's Record-Low Fertility Rate

Singapore's resident **Total Fertility Rate fell to a record low of 0.87 in 2025**, down from 0.97 in 2024, while only around **27,500 resident babies** were born. The Government has described the demographic situation as an **"existential challenge"**. Low fertility is linked to wider factors such as delayed marriage, financial and housing concerns, career aspirations, childcare responsibilities and changing attitudes towards parenthood.

**Use:** This supports the argument that declining fertility is not simply the result of individuals being unwilling to have children, but reflects wider economic, workplace and social conditions that can make marriage and parenthood more difficult.
