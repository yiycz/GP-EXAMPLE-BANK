## Workplace Fairness Act

Singapore’s **Workplace Fairness Act**, passed in 2025, prohibits adverse employment decisions based on protected characteristics such as age, nationality, sex, pregnancy, caregiving responsibilities, race, religion, disability and mental-health conditions. Employers will also be required to establish grievance-handling processes and will be prohibited from retaliating against workers who raise genuine grievances. As of **September 2026**, the legislation has been passed but is not yet in force, with implementation targeted for the **end of 2027**.

**Use:** This supports the argument that governments can strengthen workplace equality by moving beyond voluntary guidelines towards statutory protection against discrimination and unfair treatment.
