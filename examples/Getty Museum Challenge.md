## Getty Museum Challenge

The getting museum's #GettyMuseumChallenge in 2020 invited people worldwide to recreate famous artworks using household objects and share them on social media. Garnering over 100,000 recreations by May, this initiative illustrates how digital platforms can cultivate virtual communities where art enthusiasts collectively appreciate and celebrate artistic expressions. 


**Use:** The proliferation of social media establishes a space where art enthusiasts can come to discuss, appreciate and celebrate art virtually.
