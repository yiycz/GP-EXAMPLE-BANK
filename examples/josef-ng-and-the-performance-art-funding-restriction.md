## Josef Ng and the performance-art funding restriction

After artist Josef Ng protested media coverage of an anti-gay operation through a controversial performance, forum theatre and performance art ceased to receive state funding in Singapore from 1994. Funding resumed in 2004.

**Use:** This supports the argument that censorship can stifle creativity and intellectual activity, depriving society of art that could stimulate critical thought.
