## Uganda's Foreign Aid Corruption Scandal (2012)

In 2012, approximately US$12.7 million in foreign aid intended for post-war reconstruction and poverty alleviation in Uganda was embezzled through the Office of the Prime Minister, prompting countries including the UK, Ireland and Norway to suspend aid.

**Use:** Corruption where the local authorities take advantage of the largesse of foreign antions and aid groups to take a cut of the direct aid provided.
