## Singapore’s Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI

In 2026, Singapore introduced a **Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI** to address the risks posed by increasingly autonomous AI systems. The framework emphasises that organisations should assess risks, constrain the powers of AI agents and preserve meaningful human accountability. For high-stakes or irreversible actions, appropriate human review should remain in place.

**Use:** This supports the argument that technological progress should remain subordinate to human judgement and responsibility, especially when autonomous systems can make consequential decisions.
