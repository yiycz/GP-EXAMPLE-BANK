## Expanded Family Support under NDR 2026

At the **2026 National Day Rally**, Singapore announced a broader approach to supporting families throughout a child’s upbringing rather than concentrating assistance mainly around birth. Measures included almost **S$70,000 in direct support for each Singaporean child**, a target of reducing government-supported full-day childcare fees to **S$150 per month**, and additional BTO ballot chances for first-timer families for every child they have or are expecting.

**Use:** This supports the argument that governments can intervene actively to reduce the financial burden of raising children, encourage family formation and address declining fertility through a combination of welfare and housing support.
