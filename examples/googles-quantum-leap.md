## Google's Quantum Leap

In December 2024, Google unveiled Willow, a quantum chip that reduced errors as it grew larger. It completed a test task in five minutes that Google said would take an ordinary supercomputer longer than the universe has existed. The benchmark was selected to suit the machine, and a broadly useful quantum computer may still be years or decades away.

**Use:** This supports the argument that a scientific breakthrough is not a finished practical tool: genuine progress can be slow and limited even when headline results appear to conquer the impossible.
