## Trump's Falling Approval Ahead of the 2026 Midterm Elections

Ahead of the **3 November 2026 US midterm elections**, President **Donald Trump's approval rating fell to a record low of 31% on 19 October**, before recovering slightly to **32% on 26 October**, while a Reuters/Ipsos poll found that **47% of registered voters supported Democrats compared with 36% supporting Republicans** in congressional elections. Widespread dissatisfaction with **inflation and rising petrol prices**, exacerbated by US military strikes on Iran, contributed to the political backlash, with **73% of respondents believing the cost of living was heading in the wrong direction**.

**Use:** Public expectations and electoral scrutiny can hold governments accountable for their economic performance, compelling political leaders to remain responsive to citizens' concerns rather than becoming complacent.
