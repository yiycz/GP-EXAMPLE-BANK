## #PizzaGate 

During the 2016 United States Presidential Election, false allegations linking Hillary Clinton to a child sex ring and misuse of a private email server spread rapidly online through the hashtag #PizzaGate. Despite lacking evidence and being later debunked, the misinformation damaged her public image and reduced voter support.


**Use:** People cannot believe what they see in the news today, due to developments in technology that has led to the proliferation of fake news.
