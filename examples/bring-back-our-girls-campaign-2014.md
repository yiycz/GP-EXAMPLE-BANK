## Bring Back Our Girls campaign (2014)

After Boko Haram abducted 276 Nigerian schoolgirls in 2014, #BringBackOurGirls attracted participation from millions of social-media users and public figures, but attention faded while many girls remained captive for years.

**Use:** This supports the argument that viral activism can generate immense awareness yet fail to sustain the long-term pressure required to solve a complex problem.
