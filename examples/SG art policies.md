## SG art policies

1. Catch.sg is a one-stop platform on which people can discover arts and cultural events, received personalised recommendations and purchase tickers. Smaller arts groups can also promote their events free of charge on the platform. This enables them to gain more visibility and reach wider audiences.

2. Poems on the MRT displays 100 poems snippets on trains and stations of the SMRT operated North-South, East-West and Circle lines. "People will see that, actually, poetry is not something esoteric. It is something that you can engage on a daily basis", as SingLit Chairperson Ms Fiona Chan puts it. 

3. NAC Creation Grant: offer up to 50,000 SG Dollars per applicant. E.g. Malay's Sketches received S$17,000. 

4. Singapore Art Week 2026, featuring over 100 art events across SG, including exhibitions, art fails, public installations and workshops. E.g. Light to Night Singapore 2026  Transformed the Civic District with illuminated artworks, projection mapping and participatory installations, making visual arts accessible in public spaces.

5. SG writers Festival bring together both domestic and international writers, to celebrate the art of storytelling and discuss new ideas. This year's theme is on "Hidden in Plain Sight" which features crime and mystery fiction. 

6. WILD Rice received support under NAC's Major Company Scheme. 

7. Dancing the algorithm is an experimental dance project supported by NAC, use AI and motion-capture technology to preserve dancers' movements and generate innovative choreography. 

8. NAC sets Key performance Indicator for its award and funding recipients which makes it restrictive for artists to pursue it. Artists who cannot meet the requirements would face daunting BTE and resort to palatable ambitions, thereby sidelining their artistic aspirations. 


**Use:** The arts are promoted through government fundings and support.
