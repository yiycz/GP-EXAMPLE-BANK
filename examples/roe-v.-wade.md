## Roe v. Wade Misinformation

An example is the false claim spread on social media that former US President Joe Biden supported the overturning of Roe v. Wade. In reality, Biden opposed the decision, which ended federal protection for abortion rights. The misinformation caused confusion at a time when many Americans were already protesting the ruling.



**Use:** Social media leads to propagation of falsehoods online. Over the years, despite extensive efforts made to rid online spaces of such malpractices, social media has unfortunately become an insidious breeding ground for the dissemination of falsehoods, blurring the line between fact and fiction.
