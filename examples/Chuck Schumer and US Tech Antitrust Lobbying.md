## Chuck Schumer and US Tech Antitrust Lobbying

Lawmakers across the political spectrum in the United States have faced criticism for scaling back their anti-monopoly or regulatory platforms due to intense lobbying from tech giants like Amazon, Apple, Google, and Meta.

Senate Majority Leader Chuck Schumer blocked bipartisan antitrust bills targeting tech conglomerates from reaching a floor vote following intense corporate lobbying.

**Use:** By quietly stalling accountability measures, an elected leader protected corporate monopolies at the expense of consumer protection. This shows how even leaders who once presented themselves as champions of the public can end up functioning as villains, not through open corruption, but by quietly protecting the powerful at the expense of those they were elected to serve.
