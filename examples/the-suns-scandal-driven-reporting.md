## The Sun's scandal-driven reporting

Britain's The Sun publishes scandals and rumours about celebrities, sports stars and other public figures, often highlighting the less savoury aspects of a situation to attract readers.

**Use:** This supports the argument that commercial pressure can encourage sensationalism, causing media organisations to sacrifice fairness, privacy and accuracy for higher readership.
