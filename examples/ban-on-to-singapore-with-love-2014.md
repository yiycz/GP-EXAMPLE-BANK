## Ban on To Singapore, with Love (2014)

Tan Pin Pin's documentary about Singaporean political exiles, To Singapore, with Love, was banned locally in 2014 because it was said to undermine national security. The ban attracted Singaporeans to screenings in Malaysia and London.

**Use:** This supports the argument that censorship can be self-defeating because restrictions heighten curiosity and encourage audiences to circumvent them through overseas or alternative sources.
