## COP28

At COP28 in Dubai in 2023, nearly 200 countries reached the UAE Consensus to transition away from fossil fuels following intense negotiations between oil-producing nations and countries advocating a complete fossil-fuel phase-out. 

However it is ineffective in delivering tangible environmental outcomes remains limited. The International Energy Agency's 2025 assessment found that national renewable energy plans still fell short of the COP28 target, demonstrating a gap between international commitments and domestic action

**Use:** Although COP28 successfully reconciled conflicting national interests and established ambitious international commitments, its effectiveness remains constrained by inadequate implementation, as renewable energy expansion remains below the required trajectory and global warming projections continue to exceed the Paris Agreement's targets.
