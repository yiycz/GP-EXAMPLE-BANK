## Lively Places Programme – NAC Partnership

In 2023, the National Arts Council partnered with HDB and URA’s **Lively Places Programme** to support community-created arts projects in everyday public spaces such as housing estates and neighbourhoods. Projects involving artists can receive **up to S$30,000 in support**, including artist fees and implementation funding, while applicants are expected to involve the local community in creating the project.

**Use:** This supports the argument that governments can make the arts more accessible by bringing cultural activities into everyday community spaces, while simultaneously creating paid opportunities for artists and encouraging public participation.
