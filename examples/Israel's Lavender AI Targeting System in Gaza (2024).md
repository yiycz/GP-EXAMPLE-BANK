## Israel's Lavender AI Targeting System in Gaza (2024)

In **April 2024**, an investigation by **+972 Magazine and Local Call**, based on interviews with six Israeli intelligence officers, alleged that the **Israel Defense Forces (IDF)** had used an AI-powered system called **Lavender** to identify approximately **37,000 Palestinians as suspected militants** for potential airstrikes, with officers reportedly spending as little as **20 seconds reviewing individual targets**, despite an estimated **10% error rate**. The investigation further alleged that military procedures permitted **15–20 civilian casualties when targeting certain low-ranking militants**, raising serious concerns about violations of **jus in bello principles of distinction, proportionality and precautions in attack**, although the **IDF disputed the allegations**, maintaining that human officers independently verified targets and assessed expected civilian harm.

**Use:** The pursuit of military objectives does not justify indiscriminate or disproportionate attacks, as belligerents remain obligated under international humanitarian law to distinguish civilians from combatants, minimise civilian casualties and ensure that anticipated civilian harm is not excessive relative to the concrete and direct military advantage expected.
