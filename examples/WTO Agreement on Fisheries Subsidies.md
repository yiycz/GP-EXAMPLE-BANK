## WTO Agreement on Fisheries Subsidies

On 15 September 2025, the WTO Agreement on Fisheries Subsidies entered into force after securing acceptance from over two-thirds of WTO members, establishing legally binding restrictions on subsidies contributing to illegal fishing and overexploitation of fish stocks.

**Use:** International organisations facilitate collective environmental action by coordinating states' resources and establishing common rules to address transnational environmental challenges that individual nations cannot effectively tackle alone.
