## Just Stop Oil and the Soup Stunt

In 2022 Just Stop Oil activists  threw soup on Van Gogh’s Sunflowers (behind glass) and then glued themselves to the wall. The clip drew nearly 50 million views, and a wave of public anger.

**Use:** This supports the argument that viral shock tactics can raise an issue and damage its support at once. It’s a provocative example of using art’s publicity to address climate change, raising responsibility debates.
