## Alfian Sa'at's Cook a pot of curry

Alfian Sa'at's Cook a pot of curry restaged 2011 "curry dispute" betweem a Chinese immigrant family and their Indian neighbour, collecting testimonies gathering from multiple perspectives to give multiple communities a platform to examine xenophobic and cultural issues in Singapore 

**Use:** The arts serve as a powerful form of social commentary that highlights social issues and raises public awareness of them.
