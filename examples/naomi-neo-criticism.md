## Naomi Neo Criticism

A case in point is Naomi Neo, one of Singapore’s most prominent influencers. Through her social media platforms, she frequently shares luxury purchases, high-end travel experiences, and glimpses of her affluent lifestyle with her large following. Such content often attracts substantial engagement and admiration, especially from younger audiences. When viewers internalise these curated portrayals of wealth and success, they may develop unrealistic expectations and become less satisfied with their own lives.



**Use:** As affluent influencers flaunt excessive spending and lavish living, viewers may feel pressured to emulate them, leading to growing dissatisfaction with their own ordinary lives.
