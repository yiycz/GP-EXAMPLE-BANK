## Singapore's Rapidly Ageing Population

Singapore's population is ageing rapidly. In **2025, 20.7% of Singapore citizens were aged 65 and above**, compared with 13.1% in 2015, while the proportion aged 20 to 64 declined over the same period. Population ageing therefore creates interconnected pressures involving healthcare demand, caregiving needs, public expenditure and a shrinking working-age population supporting a growing elderly population.

**Use:** This supports the argument that population ageing is not merely an elderly-welfare issue, but a structural demographic challenge affecting healthcare, families, the labour force and public finances.
