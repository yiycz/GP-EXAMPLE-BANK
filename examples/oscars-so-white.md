## Oscars So White

The hashtag **#OscarsSoWhite** emerged in 2015 and gained renewed attention in 2016 after all 20 acting nominees at the Academy Awards were white. The controversy sparked wider criticism of racial underrepresentation in Hollywood and raised questions about who holds influence in the film industry, whose stories receive recognition, and which groups are given opportunities to participate in cultural production.

**Use:** This supports the argument that art and cultural institutions can reinforce social inequalities when recognition and influence are concentrated among particular groups, while greater diversity can broaden the range of stories and perspectives represented in society.
