## Procreate

Contemporary software such as Procreate provides professional-grade capabilities for little to no cost, effectively consolidating a multi-thousand-dollar physical studio into ubiquitous consumer hardware like smartphones and tablets.

**Use:** The democratization of artistic expression through digital tools has drastically minimized the financial and physical barriers historically associated with creative production, thereby empowering individuals regardless of their socio-economic backgrounds.
