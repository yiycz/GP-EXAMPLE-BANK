## POFMA direction against Goh Meng Seng (2024)

In 2024, Singapore issued a POFMA Correction Direction to opposition politician Goh Meng Seng after he claimed that a businessman had received a major LTA contract during S. Iswaran's tenure as Transport Minister.

**Use:** This supports the argument that correction directions allow governments to respond rapidly to disputed online claims without immediately removing the original post.
