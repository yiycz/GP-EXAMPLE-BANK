## Anthony Chen's Ilo Ilo

Anthony Chen's Ilo Ilo told the story of a Filipino domestic worker and the family she worked for, promoting many Singaporeans to reflect on how foreign workers who are often invisible in daily life are actually treated in their homes. 

**Use:** The arts can create meaningful social change by building understanding between groups with different cultural identities, experiences or perspectives. It also shows that the arts can present voice of underrepresented communities in society.
