## AI Goes to War

Reports in 2024 described Israel's use of an AI system called Lavender to flag suspected militants during the Gaza war. At one point it listed about 37,000 Palestinians as potential targets, while human officers reportedly spent only seconds checking many choices despite a known error rate. Israel disputes parts of the reporting.

**Use:** This supports the argument that data-driven warfare can overlook human values: when officers merely rubber-stamp an algorithm's life-or-death recommendation, speed replaces judgement and the machine effectively decides.
