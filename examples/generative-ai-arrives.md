## Generative AI Arrives

ChatGPT launched in November 2022 and reached 100 million users within about two months, the fastest a consumer app had ever grown. The chatbot can write essays, code and emails from a simple prompt, giving students, workers and writers a powerful new tool. The same fluency can also flood the internet with fakes, and ChatGPT can invent false facts with total confidence.

**Use:** This supports the argument that generative AI is genuinely powerful but deeply flawed: it offers useful help at extraordinary scale while making misinformation and the outsourcing of human thought much easier.
