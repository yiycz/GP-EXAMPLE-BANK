## Singapore's National AI Strategy 2.0 (2023–2026)

On **4 December 2023**, Singapore launched its **National AI Strategy 2.0 (NAIS 2.0)** to position itself as a global AI hub and harness artificial intelligence for economic growth and public welfare, aiming to **triple its pool of AI practitioners to 15,000** while strengthening AI adoption in sectors such as healthcare, education and finance. In **2026**, the government established a **National AI Council chaired by Prime Minister Lawrence Wong**, committed **over S$1 billion to AI research and talent development from 2025 to 2030**, and launched the **National AI Impact Programme to support 10,000 enterprises over three years**, demonstrating Singapore's proactive efforts to develop technological capabilities and maintain economic competitiveness.

**Use:** Governments can play a crucial role in promoting technological advancement by coordinating national resources, developing human capital and facilitating innovation, thereby strengthening economic competitiveness and preparing society for technological disruption.
