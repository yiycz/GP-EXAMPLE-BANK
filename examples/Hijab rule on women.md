## Hijab rule on women

Countries such as Iran and Saudi Arabia impose a mandatory hijab rule on women, such that a refusal to wear the headscarf puts a woman in legal jeopardy. Moreover, Iranian authorities installed cameras in public places to identity unveiled women. 

**Use:** Freedom is a basic human right for individuals to make his decisions as long as it does not infringe on the rights of others.
