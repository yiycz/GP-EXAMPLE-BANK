## Tanzania's Foreign Aid Success

By 2025, Tanzania's World Bank-supported Productive Social Safety Net programme, which combined immediate cash transfers with business grants and entrepreneurship training, had helped approximately 400,000 households graduate from welfare assistance, with the Tanzanian government reporting in June 2026 that these households could sustain their livelihoods independently.


**Use:** Foreign aid acts as a stop-gap measure to help countries in the short run, and nations can build up their capacity achievement developments.
