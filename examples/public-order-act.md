## Public Order Act

Under Singapore’s **Public Order Act**, a police permit is generally required for public assemblies or processions intended to demonstrate support or opposition, publicise a cause or commemorate an event. **Speakers’ Corner at Hong Lim Park** provides an exception under specified conditions, although restrictions on participation and certain racial or religious matters remain.

**Use:** This supports the argument that Singapore prioritises public order and social stability over an unrestricted right to protest, illustrating the tension between effective governance and civil liberties. This is paternalistic because the Government **restricts individual freedom to protest in order to preserve public order, stability and social harmony**.
