## EU–Tunisia migration agreement 

In 2023, the EU agreed to provide Tunisia with €105 million in migration-related assistance to strengthen border controls and curb migration towards Europe, despite documented abuses against migrants by Tunisian security forces. In July 2026, Amnesty International criticised the arrangement for contributing to serious human rights violations.


**Use:** Foreign aid may be ineffective in promoting genuine development because donor countries can exploit the economic vulnerabilities of recipient nations by attaching conditions that serve their own national interests.
