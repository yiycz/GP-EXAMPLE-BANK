## GST Voucher Scheme

The government provides lower- and middle-income Singaporeans with financial assistance through cash payouts, MediSave top-ups and utility rebates, including up to S$850 in GST Voucher cash payments for eligible adults in August 2026.

The scheme helps mitigate the regressive impact of GST and rising living costs, benefiting approximately 1.5 million adults through cash payouts in 2026, although financial transfers provide limited long-term protection against persistent inflation.
