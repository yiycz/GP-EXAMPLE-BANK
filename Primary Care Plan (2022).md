## Primary Care Plan (2022)

The government mandated affordable primary healthcare coverage for migrant workers living in dormitories or employed in construction, marine shipyard and process industries, providing access to medical consultations, chronic disease treatment and health screenings.

The scheme has demonstrated considerable success, with over 96% of surveyed migrant workers satisfied with healthcare services and 92% reporting convenient access to medical care, although it does not directly address wider issues of workplace exploitation and social exclusion.
