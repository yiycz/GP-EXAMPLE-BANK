## SG Green Plan

SG Green Plan seeks to galvanize a whole-of-nation movement and advance SG's national agenda on sustainable developments. 

One example is tackling sea levels, which requires the state's involvement as the infrastructure needs huge investment. Government puts aside 10 billion dollars to invest in R&D to dea with the problem. 

Planting 1 million trees -> embark on an ambitious programme to plant 1 million trees by the end of 2030 with a vision of having a "City in nature".

**Use:** Governments possess the resources and levers of government to implement policies to benefit society and bring about necessary changes.
