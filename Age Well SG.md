## Age Well SG

The government committed S$3.5 billion over a decade to expand Active Ageing Centres (AACs), improve elderly-friendly infrastructure and strengthen community care services, enabling seniors to age independently.

The initiative has made commendable progress, with over 230 AACs established and 8 in 10 seniors having access to a centre near their homes, thereby improving opportunities for social interaction and reducing the risk of elderly isolation, although accessibility does not guarantee participation.
