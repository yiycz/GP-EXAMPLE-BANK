## Expansion of BTO Housing Supply

Description: The government committed to launching approximately 55,000 BTO flats from 2025 to 2027, including shorter-waiting-time flats, to meet housing demand and help young couples settle down earlier. 

Effectiveness: The expansion has improved accessibility, with median application rates for first-time families falling from nearly seven applicants per flat in 2020 to between 1.1 and 1.9 in 2025, although construction waiting times remain a constraint.
