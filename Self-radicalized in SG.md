## Self-radicalized in SG

Several young people have been detained by the Internet Security Act due to their Internet activities which revealed their intention to join horror group or support their jihadist cause. 
A case saw a young teenage girl at 15, intending to go to Syria to join ISIS, the Islamic terrorist group and become a "jihadist" bride by marrying a ISIS soldier. She was detained by the government.
